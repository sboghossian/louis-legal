Draft a cover letter for a patent application submission.

# Required inputs
- Applicant (and inventors)
- Title of invention
- Jurisdiction (USPTO, EPO, WIPO PCT, regional office, MENA national office)
- Type: utility / design / plant
- Priority claim (if claiming priority from earlier filing within 12 months)
- Power of Attorney for foreign counsel

# Content
1. Identification of applicant + inventors with addresses
2. Title of the invention
3. Type of filing (e.g., "non-provisional utility patent application")
4. Priority claim with earlier-filing details
5. List of accompanying documents (specification, claims, drawings, abstract, oath/declaration, fees)
6. POA / authorization
7. Express statement of permitted publication (or request for non-publication if available)
8. Filing fee tender

# Jurisdictional notes
- **USPTO**: USC 35; non-publication request available if foreign filing not contemplated
- **EPO**: European Patent Convention; designation of states
- **PCT**: WIPO international filing; designates contracting states
- **MENA**:
  - GCC Patent Office (Riyadh): regional protection
  - Saudi SAIP: national filings
  - UAE: federal MOEC patents office
  - LB: MOET intellectual property office

# Best practice
- File priority claim early — 12-month window is hard
- Use professional patent attorney for claim drafting; AI assists with the cover/structural materials but claim language demands specialist input
