# UAE Tax Law — Quick Reference (as of 2026)

## Corporate Tax (Federal Decree-Law 47/2022, effective June 2023)
- Standard rate: **9%** on taxable income above AED 375,000
- Below AED 375,000: 0%
- Multinational top-up tax (DMTT — Pillar Two): 15% for MNEs >€750M revenue (effective 2025)
- **Free zone**: qualifying activities still 0% if QFZP (Qualifying Free Zone Person) conditions met
- Tax registration mandatory; returns annual

## VAT (Federal Decree-Law 8/2017, effective Jan 2018)
- Standard rate: **5%**
- Zero-rated: exports, international transport, certain medical/educational, residential first supply
- Exempt: bare land, residential rentals, local passenger transport, certain financial services
- Registration threshold: AED 375,000 mandatory; AED 187,500 voluntary

## Excise Tax
- Tobacco + e-cigarettes: 100%
- Energy drinks: 100%
- Sweetened beverages: 50%
- Carbonated drinks: 50%

## Personal income tax
- **None** (no individual income tax)
- No capital gains tax for individuals on personal investments
- Real estate gains may trigger CT if held by a company

## Withholding tax
- Generally none
- Specific cases: 0% on most cross-border payments (favorable for international groups)

## Transfer pricing
- OECD-aligned rules (Decree 47/2022)
- Documentation requirements for related-party transactions
- Country-by-country reporting for large MNEs

## Free zones (corporate tax)
- 50+ free zones in UAE
- Qualifying activities for QFZP status retained 0% rate
- Non-qualifying income subject to 9%

## DIFC + ADGM
- Corporate tax: subject to UAE federal CT
- Specific exemptions for certain financial activities

## Family offices + funds
- Specific exemptions / structures available
- Qualifying Investment Fund status

## Anti-avoidance
- General anti-abuse rules (GAAR)
- Specific anti-avoidance for certain structures
