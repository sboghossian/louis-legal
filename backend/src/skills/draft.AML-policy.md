Draft an Anti-Money Laundering (AML) policy for a financial-services or fintech business.

# Required inputs
- Entity scope (which group entities covered)
- Regulatory jurisdiction (FATF aligned; specific local requirements)
- Business model (which transactions, customer types, geographies)
- Reporting officer (MLRO) designation

# Mandatory components
1. **Policy statement** — commitment to AML/CFT compliance
2. **MLRO appointment** — Money Laundering Reporting Officer (or equivalent)
3. **Risk-based approach** — customer risk categorization (low/medium/high)
4. **Customer Due Diligence (CDD)**:
   - Identification (ID document verification)
   - Beneficial ownership (25%+ threshold typical)
   - Purpose of relationship
   - Source of funds for high-risk
5. **Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)** — PEPs, high-risk jurisdictions, cash-intensive
6. **Ongoing monitoring** — transaction patterns, threshold-based alerts, periodic reviews
7. **Sanctions screening** — against OFAC, UN, EU, local sanctions lists; daily updates
8. **Suspicious Activity Reports (SAR/STR)** — filing thresholds + processes
9. **Record retention** — typically 5-7 years
10. **Training** — annual mandatory for all staff; specialized for compliance team
11. **Audit + testing** — independent annual review

# Jurisdictional alignment
- **FATF Recommendations** — global baseline (40 Recommendations)
- **KSA**: SAMA Rules, FIO (Financial Investigation Office)
- **UAE Federal**: Federal Decree-Law 20/2018, goAML reporting
- **DIFC**: DFSA AML Module
- **ADGM**: FSRA AML Rulebook
- **LB**: BDL Special Investigation Commission, Law 44/2015
- **EU**: 6th AML Directive
- **US**: BSA, FinCEN

# Critical for fintech
- **VARA, ADGM Crypto regimes** — additional virtual-asset-specific obligations
- **Travel Rule** for crypto transfers (FATF Recommendation 16)
- **Real-time monitoring** + automated alerting expected

# Implementation checklist
- MLRO appointed + reporting line
- CDD procedures documented + executed
- Sanctions screening tool operational
- Transaction monitoring tool operational
- Staff training delivered + records
- Annual independent review
