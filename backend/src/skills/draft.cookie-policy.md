Draft a Cookie Policy with consent mechanism aligned to applicable regime.

# Categories of cookies to enumerate
1. **Strictly necessary** — no consent required under GDPR/ePrivacy (auth, session, security)
2. **Functional** — preferences, language; consent needed if non-essential
3. **Analytics** — Google Analytics, Mixpanel, PostHog; consent required
4. **Advertising/Marketing** — third-party tracking; explicit opt-in
5. **Social media** — embedded widgets

# Standard sections
1. What cookies are (1-paragraph plain English)
2. Why we use them (purposes)
3. Categories table (each cookie: provider, purpose, duration, type)
4. Consent mechanism + how to change preferences
5. Third-party cookies + links to provider policies
6. Updates and contact

# Jurisdictional notes
- **EU + UK (ePrivacy + GDPR)**: opt-in consent required for non-essential cookies; granular categories
- **California (CCPA)**: opt-out for "sale of personal info"; "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link
- **UAE/KSA**: increasingly aligned with consent-based frameworks
- **LB**: no codified cookie law but GDPR best-practice often applied for international visitor compliance
