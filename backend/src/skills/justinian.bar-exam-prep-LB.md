Lebanese bar exam preparation — the *concours d'avocat* + stage requirements.

# Overview
- Entry to Bar requires 3 years of legal studies + bar admission process
- Bar entry exam (concours) varies by Bar Association (Beirut, Tripoli, regional)
- After concours: 3-year stage as avocat stagiaire under a tuteur

# Beirut Bar Association concours (most common path)
- **Written exams**: civil procedure, commercial law, civil law, penal law, ethics
- **Oral exam**: defense argument, legal-knowledge interview
- Typically held annually
- Strong competition

# Subjects covered
- **Lebanese Civil Code** (Code des Obligations et des Contrats)
- **Commercial Code**
- **Penal Code + Code of Penal Procedure**
- **Personal Status Laws** (multi-confessional)
- **Labor Law**
- **Civil Procedure Code**
- **Bar Ethics + Code of Conduct**

# Study approach
- Code-based memorization (Lebanese law is civil-law / code-based)
- Case examples (cassation jurisprudence)
- Procedural fluency (timelines, deadlines, forms)
- Bilingual command (French / Arabic / both)

# Time investment
- 6-12 months focused study typical
- Practice papers + mock orals
- Study groups effective

# Justinian-specific support
- Flashcards on each Code article
- IRAC-coaching on past concours questions
- Oral-defense rehearsal
- Procedural deadline coach

# After bar entry
- 3-year stage required
- Some advocacy work permitted during stage
- Full inscription as avocat after stage + final exam
