# UAE Corporate Law — Quick Reference

## Primary sources
- Federal Decree-Law 32/2021 (Commercial Companies Law) — replaced 2015 law
- Cabinet Decisions implementing it (e.g., Cabinet Decision 55/2021 strategic sectors)
- Free zone-specific regulations (DIFC, ADGM, Dubai Multi Commodities Centre, etc.)
- Federal Decree-Law 47/2022 (Corporate Tax) — separate but affects all entities

## Entity types
### Onshore (mainland)
- **LLC (شركة ذات مسؤولية محدودة)** — 100% foreign ownership permitted since 2021 reform (most sectors)
- **Private Joint Stock Company (PJSC)** — 3+ shareholders; min capital AED 5M
- **Public Joint Stock Company** — listed on DFM/ADX
- **Sole proprietorship**
- **Civil company** — for licensed professions
- **Branch of foreign company**

### Free zones (>40 across UAE)
- **DIFC** (Dubai International Financial Centre) — common-law regime; financial services
- **ADGM** (Abu Dhabi Global Market) — common-law regime; financial + crypto
- **DMCC** (Dubai Multi Commodities Centre) — trade
- **JAFZA** (Jebel Ali Free Zone) — logistics
- **Dubai Silicon Oasis** — tech
- Many others — specific industry focus

## Foreign ownership
- Mainland: 100% allowed since June 2021 (with sector exclusions per Cabinet Decision 55/2021)
- Strategic sectors still restricted (banking, insurance, telecoms, oil)
- Free zones: 100% foreign always permitted

## Incorporation onshore (Dubai DED example)
1. Trade name reservation
2. Initial approval (Department of Economic Development)
3. MOA / AOA drafting + notarization (notary public)
4. Office tenancy (Ejari registration)
5. Trade license issuance
6. Chamber of Commerce membership
7. UBO + economic substance filings
8. Corporate Tax registration (FTA)

## Incorporation in free zone (DIFC example)
1. Application to DIFC Authority
2. Articles of Association in DIFC standard form
3. License fee
4. Office space (physical or shared via business center)
5. DFSA license if regulated activity
6. DIFC Courts jurisdiction

## Governance
- LLC: Managers (1+); General Assembly
- PJSC: Board (5-15 directors)
- Annual general assembly
- Statutory audit thresholds

## Beneficial Ownership
- Mandatory UBO register (>25% threshold)
- Filing with relevant authority (DED, free zone, FTA)
- Penalties for non-compliance

## Economic Substance (ESR)
- Cabinet Decision 31/2019 + 57/2020
- Applies to relevant activities (banking, insurance, IP, holding, etc.)
- Annual reporting + substance demonstration

## Recent reforms
- Federal Decree-Law 32/2021 — Commercial Companies Law
- Cabinet Decision 55/2021 — strategic sectors list
- Federal Decree-Law 47/2022 — Corporate Tax (9% standard, 0% under 375K AED)
- 2024 implementing regulations for free zones
