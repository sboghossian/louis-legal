Reseller agreement structure:
- Distinct from distribution: typically B2B SaaS / digital products
- Margin / commission
- Restrictions: marketing + sales territory
- Co-branded vs Franchisor-branded sales
- Customer relationship ownership (Franchisor typically retains)
- Termination + transition
