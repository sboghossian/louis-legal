Draft an employment termination letter.

# Required inputs
- Employer + Employee
- Effective termination date
- Grounds (cause / without cause / mutual / resignation)
- Notice or pay in lieu
- Final settlement (last salary, accrued leave, EOSG/EOSA, ex gratia)
- Return of property
- Any restrictive covenants on the employee post-termination

# Pattern
1. Identification of parties + date
2. Formal notice of termination (cite contract clause + statutory basis)
3. Effective date
4. Grounds (if for cause: specific factual basis — avoid characterizations without evidence)
5. Notice period or pay in lieu calculation
6. Final-settlement amounts itemized
7. Conditions: return of property, confidentiality continuation, non-disparagement
8. Reference letter terms (if agreed)
9. Acknowledgment / receipt mechanism

# Jurisdictional notes
- **UAE federal**: termination procedure under Decree-Law 33/2021; strict grounds for cause; arbitrary dismissal exposes employer to compensation
- **KSA**: Article 80 for cause termination; specific grounds + procedural requirements; arbitrary dismissal compensation set by statute
- **LB**: Labor Code Art 50 (procedural) and Art 74 (grounds for cause); strict notice formula
- **DIFC**: DIFC Employment Law Art 32+; statutory minimum notice; broader contractual flexibility

# Critical
- **Don't characterize misconduct beyond what's documented** — risk of defamation claims
- **Statutory minimums cannot be waived** — pay in lieu/EOSG even where employer "for cause"
- **Sponsor cancellation** for non-citizens in MENA — coordinate with visa cancellation timing
- **Mass redundancies** trigger additional consultation in some jurisdictions

Use alongside [[draft.severance-agreement]] for a fuller exit pack.
