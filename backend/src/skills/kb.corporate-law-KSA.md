# KSA Corporate Law — Quick Reference

## Primary sources
- Companies Law (Royal Decree M/132 2022) — major reform replacing 2015 law
- Ministry of Commerce (MOC) implementing regulations
- Capital Market Authority (CMA) for listed companies
- Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) for financial institutions

## Entity types (post-2022 reform)
- **Joint Stock Company (شركة مساهمة)** — JSC; min 2 shareholders (was 5); listed or unlisted
- **Limited Liability Company (شركة ذات مسؤولية محدودة)** — LLC; min 1 shareholder (single-member allowed); cap-table flexibility increased
- **Simplified Joint Stock Company (شركة مساهمة مبسطة)** — NEW under 2022 reform; flexible governance for startups
- **General Partnership (شركة تضامن)**
- **Limited Partnership (شركة توصية بسيطة)**
- **Holding Company structures** — allowed
- **Professional Civil Companies** — for licensed professions

## Foreign ownership
- Foreign Investment Law (Royal Decree M/1 2000, reformed 2025): 100% foreign ownership permitted in most sectors
- Restricted: oil exploration, certain media, military, holy cities real estate
- SAGIA / MISA investment license required for foreign-owned entities
- Regional headquarters (RHQ) license — special privileges for MNCs

## Incorporation procedure
1. **MISA** investment license (foreign-owned)
2. Name reservation + Articles via MOC e-services
3. Capital deposit + bank account
4. Commercial Registration (CR) issuance
5. Chamber of Commerce membership
6. Tax + Zakat registration with ZATCA
7. GOSI + Qiwa registration for employees
8. Municipal license for physical premises

## Governance
- JSC: minimum 3-board members
- LLC: 1+ manager
- Annual general assembly
- Sharia-compliant audit may be required for Islamic finance entities
- Sharia Supervisory Board for Islamic banks

## Recent reforms (2022 Companies Law highlights)
- Simplified JSC for startups
- Single-shareholder LLC
- Improved minority-protection rights
- Enhanced corporate governance code
- Beneficial ownership disclosure required

## Listing on Tadawul / Nomu
- Tadawul Main Market (for established companies)
- Nomu Parallel Market (for SMEs)
- CMA approval required

## Annual obligations
- Annual general assembly within 6 months of year-end
- Audited financial statements
- Commercial Registration renewal
- Zakat / income tax filings
