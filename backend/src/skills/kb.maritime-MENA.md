# Maritime Law in MENA — Quick Reference

## International framework
- **UNCLOS** (UN Convention on the Law of the Sea)
- **Maritime Conventions** — Hague-Visby, Hamburg, Rotterdam Rules for bills of lading
- **MARPOL** — pollution
- **SOLAS** — safety
- **MLC 2006** — Maritime Labour Convention
- **ISM Code** — International Safety Management

## Saudi Arabia
- **General Authority of Ports (Mawani)**
- **Saudi Ports Authority** — port operations
- **Saudi Maritime Code** — domestic maritime law
- Major ports: Jeddah Islamic Port, King Abdulaziz Port (Dammam), King Fahd Industrial Port (Yanbu)

## UAE
- **Federal Maritime Code** (Federal Law 26/1981 as amended)
- **Federal Maritime + Land Transport Authority**
- **Emirate-level port operators**: DP World (Jebel Ali), Abu Dhabi Ports
- DIFC Courts increasingly used for maritime arbitration

## Lebanon
- **Maritime Code (Decree-Law 4 / 1947)**
- **Port of Beirut** + **Port of Tripoli** — main commercial ports

## Egypt
- **Maritime Trade Code** + amendments
- **Suez Canal Authority** — Suez Canal operations
- **Egyptian Maritime Code** — Code de la Marine Marchande

## Common issues
- **Bill of lading** disputes
- **Charter parties** — voyage, time, demise charters
- **Cargo claims** — damage, delay, short delivery
- **Collisions + salvage**
- **Marine insurance**
- **Crew employment + injuries**
- **Port + customs**
- **Sanctions** — vessel sanctions screening crucial

## Disputes
- **London Maritime Arbitrators Association (LMAA)** — most common forum
- **DIAC, DIFC-LCIA** — increasingly used for MENA maritime
- **Local courts** — for some matters
- **Vessel arrest** procedures vary by jurisdiction

## Practical
- Hague-Visby Rules vs Hamburg Rules: which applies per BoL
- Time-bar: 1 year for cargo claims under Hague-Visby
- General Average — York-Antwerp Rules
- Marine insurance: London standard wordings common

## Suez Canal specific
- SCA rules + tariffs
- Pilotage mandatory
- Convoy system
- Insurance + war risk additional charges
