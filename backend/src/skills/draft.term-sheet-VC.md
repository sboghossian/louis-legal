Draft a non-binding term sheet for a priced VC round (Seed / Series A).

# Required inputs
- Company, founders, current cap table
- Lead investor, syndicate
- Round size, pre-money / post-money valuation
- Option pool sizing (pre or post)
- Liquidation preference (1x non-participating standard; 1x participating capped)
- Board composition (1 investor, 1 founder, 1 independent typical)
- Closing conditions and timeline

# Standard clauses to include
1. Type of security (Preferred Stock — Series A / Seed Preferred)
2. Pre-money + investment + option pool → post-money
3. Per-share price calculation
4. Conversion right (1:1 initial)
5. Anti-dilution: broad-based weighted average (standard) vs full-ratchet (founder-unfriendly)
6. Liquidation preference + participation
7. Dividends (cumulative vs non-cumulative; payable on liquidation/conversion only is typical)
8. Voting + protective provisions (list of reserved matters needing investor consent)
9. Board composition + observer rights
10. ROFR, co-sale, drag-along, tag-along
11. Information rights
12. Pro-rata participation in future rounds
13. Pre-emptive rights
14. Vesting on founder shares (4 years, 1-year cliff is standard) — including acceleration on change of control (single vs double trigger)
15. No-shop period (typically 30-45 days)
16. Conditions precedent: due diligence, legal opinion, definitive docs, board approvals
17. Expense reimbursement (capped)
18. Governing law (Delaware for US deals; DIFC/ADGM for MENA tech rounds increasingly)

# Mark clearly as NON-BINDING (except no-shop + confidentiality + expense provisions)
