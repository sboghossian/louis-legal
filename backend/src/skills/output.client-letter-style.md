Format output as a formal letter to client.

# Structure
1. **Letterhead** (firm + matter + date + RE)
2. **Greeting** ("Dear [Name]")
3. **Opening** — context + purpose of letter (1 paragraph)
4. **Analysis / advice** (plain English; technical citations footnoted or in appendix)
5. **Recommendation + next steps** (action items)
6. **Closing** ("We remain at your disposal…")
7. **Signature block**

# Voice
- More formal than internal memos
- Translate jargon to plain English on first use
- Address as professional peers (not customer-service tone)
- Confident but appropriately calibrated

# Specific cautions
- **Privilege markings** if relevant ("Privileged & Confidential")
- **Reservation of rights** when sending demand-adjacent letters
- **Reference to engagement letter** for scope
- **Avoid promises** ("we will win") — calibrate to outcomes
