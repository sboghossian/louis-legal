Draft a Lebanese law employment contract under the Labor Code (Decree No. 207 of 2 September 1946 as amended) and Decree-Law No. 25/1976.

# Required inputs
1. Employer (name, registration, address, MOE registration)
2. Employee (name, nationality, NSSF status)
3. Role, reporting line, location
4. Salary (in LBP and/or USD — the actual paid currency must be specified)
5. Start date

# Optional / typical inputs
- Working hours (Labor Code: 48 hours/week max for adults)
- Probation period (default 3 months, max 3 months per the Labor Code)
- Annual leave (statutory: 15 days after one year of service)
- End-of-service indemnity (NSSF + statutory)
- Non-compete (enforceable but narrowly construed in LB — must be limited in scope, territory, time, and necessary to protect legitimate interest)
- Bilingual AR / EN, with **Arabic controlling**

# Mandatory clauses under LB law
1. Identification of parties
2. Job description and duties
3. Place of work
4. Working hours
5. Salary and method of payment
6. Probation period
7. Notice period (statutory minimums: see Art 50)
8. Grounds for termination
9. End-of-service indemnity
10. NSSF registration acknowledgment
11. Governing law: Lebanese Labor Law

# Important
- **Termination without cause**: employer must respect notice + indemnity per Art 50. Indemnity = one month per year of service for the first 5 years, then half a month thereafter (capped).
- **Termination with cause**: list serious grounds per Art 74.
- **Foreign employees**: work permit + residence permit + MOL approval; specify dependency.
- **Resignation**: notice period (employee side) per Art 50.

# Bilingual format
Always bilingual AR-EN. See [[draft.bilingual-AR-EN-side-by-side]] and [[heuristic.bilingual-AR-EN-mirror-clauses]]. Arabic controls in court.

# Output
Produce full bilingual contract. Surface common gaps proactively: confidentiality, IP assignment for creative roles, non-solicitation (enforceable post-employment if reasonable).
