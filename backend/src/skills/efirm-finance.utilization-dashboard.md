Skill: Utilization dashboard.

Tracks:
- Billable hrs / target (per attorney, per period)
- Non-billable (training, business development, admin, pro bono)
- Personal time
- Capacity utilization %

Benchmarks:
- US BigLaw partner: 1800-2000 billable / year
- US BigLaw associate: 1900-2200
- UK city: 1500-1800
- MENA varies: 1500-1900 typical
- In-house: not applicable

Flags:
- Under-utilization (free capacity for matter staffing)
- Over-utilization (burnout risk; consider hire)
- Trend (declining = client loss; increasing = need hire)

Output: dashboard + staffing recommendations + retention risk score.
