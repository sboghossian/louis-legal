Monthly investor update format:
1. **Highlights** (3-5 bullets)
2. **Metrics** (MRR, MAU, NPS, retention, runway)
3. **Wins** (deals closed, partnerships, hires)
4. **Challenges** (open issues, asks of investors)
5. **Asks** (intros, advice, time)
6. **Looking ahead** (1-month + 1-quarter goals)

Tone: direct, honest, no spin. Investors want signal, not hype.
