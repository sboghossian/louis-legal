Draft an Eviction Notice / Notice to Vacate.

# Required inputs
- Landlord + Tenant
- Property
- Grounds for eviction (non-payment / breach / end-of-term / lawful purpose / occupier rights)
- Statutory notice period for jurisdiction + grounds
- Cure period (if statute provides for one)

# Jurisdictional notice periods (verify current law)
- **UAE-Dubai**: 12 months notice via notary or registered post for non-renewal at term-end; Decree 26/2013 art 25 governs
- **UAE-Abu Dhabi**: similar 12-month notice; Tawtheeq mandatory
- **KSA**: depends on lease type; Ejar registration controls
- **LB**: Old Rent Law: significant tenant protection, hard to evict; New Rent Law: standard 3-year term with renewal rights
- **FR**: 6 months (residential unfurnished) for landlord non-renewal at term-end
- **UK** (E&W): Section 21 (assured shorthold) or Section 8 (specific grounds)

# Structure
1. Notice header — date + addresses
2. Identification of property + lease
3. Grounds for eviction (citing lease clause and/or statute)
4. Cure period (if applicable) — e.g., 30 days to pay arrears
5. Date by which tenant must vacate
6. Consequences of non-compliance — court proceedings, costs
7. Reservation of rights
8. Signature + delivery method (registered post / bailiff / notary)

# Critical
- **Service method matters** — wrong method invalidates notice in many jurisdictions
- **Notice period miscalculation** — most common reason landlords lose
- **Specific statute citation** — strengthens the notice and creates a clear paper trail
- **Cure opportunity** — many jurisdictions require it for breach-based evictions
- **Discrimination check** — fair-housing laws apply

# Anti-pattern
Self-help eviction (changing locks, removing belongings) is illegal in virtually every jurisdiction. Always go through formal process.
