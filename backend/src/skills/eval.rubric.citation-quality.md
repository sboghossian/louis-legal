Score AI legal output on **citation quality** — are sources real, accurate, and properly cited?

# Scoring (0-5)
- **5**: All citations are real, accurately quoted, pin-cited where appropriate, formatted per applicable style (Bluebook / OSCOLA / civil-law-FR).
- **4**: All citations real but minor formatting issues or missing pin-cites.
- **3**: All real but inconsistent style or missing some pin-cites; not misleading.
- **2**: Most real but at least one questionable or wrong source.
- **1**: Mix of real and fabricated.
- **0**: Significant fabrication — invented case names, statute numbers, or pin-cites.

# Sub-criteria
- **Existence** — does the source exist?
- **Accuracy** — is the quoted/paraphrased proposition actually what the source says?
- **Pin-cite precision** — paragraph / page / section level?
- **Format** — matches applicable style?
- **Completeness** — every legal proposition has a supporting cite OR is clearly marked as general background?

# Critical
**Fabricated citations are the single most dangerous failure mode for legal AI.** This rubric is the early-warning system.
