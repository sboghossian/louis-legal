# Aviation Law in MENA — Quick Reference

## International framework
- **ICAO** — International Civil Aviation Organization
- **Chicago Convention 1944** — base of international civil aviation
- **Montreal Convention 1999** — carrier liability (replaced Warsaw)
- **Cape Town Convention** — security interests in mobile equipment (aircraft financing)
- **Bilateral Air Service Agreements** — country-to-country traffic rights

## Saudi Arabia
- **GACA** — General Authority of Civil Aviation
- **Saudi Civil Aviation Law**
- Major airlines: Saudia, flynas (LCC), Riyadh Air (new)
- Vision 2030 aviation expansion + tourism strategy

## UAE
- **GCAA** — General Civil Aviation Authority (federal)
- Emirate-level: Dubai Civil Aviation Authority + ABU DHABI AVIATION AUTHORITY
- Major airlines: Emirates, Etihad, flydubai, Air Arabia
- Major hubs: DXB (Dubai), AUH (Abu Dhabi), SHJ (Sharjah)
- Cape Town Convention member

## Lebanon
- **DGCA** — Directorate General of Civil Aviation
- Middle East Airlines (MEA) — flag carrier
- Beirut-Rafic Hariri International Airport (BEY)

## Egypt
- **Egyptian Civil Aviation Authority (ECAA)**
- EgyptAir — flag carrier
- Major hub: CAI (Cairo)

## Common issues
- **Aircraft financing + leasing** — Cape Town Convention application
- **Slot allocation + ground handling**
- **Passenger compensation** — EU 261-style claims expanding to MENA
- **Carrier liability** — Montreal Convention
- **Safety incident investigations**
- **Sanctions screening** — sanctioned aircraft + airlines
- **Drone regulation** — emerging

## Aircraft leasing
- Most major MENA airlines use sale-and-leaseback structures
- Cape Town Convention provides predictable IDERA + deregistration mechanism
- Lebanon: NOT a Cape Town signatory (lender risk)
- Most other MENA: signatories

## Aviation disputes
- **DIAC + DIFC-LCIA** — increasingly used for MENA aviation
- **English + Irish law** — common governing law for aviation finance
- **Local arbitration** — for some matters

## Cargo
- Air waybill conventions
- IATA standard documentation
- Carrier liability limits per Montreal Convention

## Major recent
- New aviation strategies: Riyadh Air, NEOM aviation
- Sustainability + SAF (sustainable aviation fuel) regulations emerging
- Climate change adaptation
