# VARA — Dubai Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority — Quick Reference

## Overview
- **VARA** — established under Law 4/2022 of Dubai
- World's first dedicated virtual-asset regulator
- Covers Dubai (excluding DIFC which has its own regime via FSRA)
- Founded 2022; rules effective February 2023

## Scope
Activities requiring VARA license:
- **Advisory Services**
- **Broker-Dealer Services**
- **Custody Services**
- **Exchange Services**
- **Lending + Borrowing Services**
- **Payments + Remittances**
- **Management + Investment Services**
- **Issuance** of virtual assets

## License categories
- **Tier 1** — Advisory / lower-risk activities
- **Tier 2** — Brokerage / custody / exchange
- **Tier 3** — Issuance + complex products
Each tier has corresponding capital requirements + governance obligations.

## Key requirements
1. **Local presence** — operating office in Dubai
2. **Capital** — minimum tier-dependent (AED 100K to multiple millions)
3. **Governance** — qualified board + senior management
4. **Compliance officer** — designated MLRO + Compliance Officer
5. **AML/CFT** — FATF-aligned obligations
6. **Cybersecurity** — VARA Cybersecurity Rulebook compliance
7. **Custody** — segregation of client + proprietary assets; insurance
8. **Reporting** — periodic financial + AML reports

## Marketing rules
- Marketing virtual assets in Dubai requires:
  - VARA-licensed entity OR registered marketer
  - Compliance with Marketing Rulebook
  - Disclaimers + risk warnings

## DIFC's separate regime
- DIFC has its own FSRA-regulated virtual asset regime
- Distinct from VARA
- Cross-licensing complex

## ADGM (Abu Dhabi) — separate again
- ADGM Crypto Asset framework predates VARA
- FSRA-regulated
- Different licensing categories

## Tokenization
- VARA covers asset-backed + utility + payment tokens
- Real estate tokenization growing focus
- NFTs covered for issuance activities

## Sanctions on stablecoins
- Stablecoin issuers face additional reserve requirements
- AED-pegged stablecoins under specific regulation

## Practical considerations
- Compliance lawyer-heavy — VARA rules are detailed + evolving
- Initial license: 3-6 months typical
- Annual fees vary by tier
- Specific reporting obligations during operation

## Recent enforcement
- Several unlicensed actors fined / banned (2024-2025)
- Enhanced cross-border cooperation
- Increasing alignment with global standards (FATF Travel Rule etc.)
