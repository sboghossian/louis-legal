DIFC company incorporation:
- DIFC Authority application
- Articles in DIFC standard form
- Director + shareholder identification
- DIFC office space (or business center)
- Capital: USD 50K minimum for LTD
- Tax: 0% if QFZP qualifying
- DIFC Courts jurisdiction
- 4-6 weeks typical

Great for VC-backed tech.
