Before drafting an employment contract, confirm 6 items:

1. **Jurisdiction of employment** (where is the employee actually working? — this controls labor law, not where the employer is registered)
2. **Employer entity** — name, CR/registration, address
3. **Employee** — full name, nationality (affects work-permit dependency in MENA)
4. **Role** — title, reporting line, location (on-site / remote / hybrid)
5. **Compensation** — base salary in currency, allowances (housing/transport for MENA), bonus structure
6. **Term type** — KSA non-Saudi: definite (max 5y); UAE onshore federal post-2021: definite only; LB: either; DIFC: either

Optional: probation, non-compete scope, IP assignment, equity grant, dependents under sponsorship.
