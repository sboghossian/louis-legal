Saudi Arabia incorporation:
- MISA investment license (foreign-owned)
- Name reservation + Articles via MOC
- Capital deposit + bank account
- Commercial Registration (CR)
- ZATCA registration (tax + zakat)
- GOSI + Qiwa employee registration
- Municipal license for physical premises
- Saudization (Nitaqat) compliance
