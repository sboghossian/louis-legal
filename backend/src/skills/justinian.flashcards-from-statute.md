Generate flashcards from a statute or law for spaced-repetition study.

# Inputs
- Statute (e.g., UAE Decree-Law 33/2021 Employment Law)
- Difficulty level
- Number of cards desired

# Card formats
1. **Term → Definition**: "Probation period (UAE)" → "Maximum 6 months per Decree-Law 33/2021 art 9"
2. **Article → Content**: "What does Decree-Law 33/2021 art 51 say?" → "EOSG: 21 days basic per year first 5 years, 30 days per year thereafter, capped at 2 years"
3. **Fact pattern → Rule**: "Employee terminated for cause after 4 years. EOSG owed?" → "Per art 44 grounds for cause termination, EOSG forfeited if grounds met; otherwise full EOSG per art 51"
4. **Comparison**: "How does UAE non-compete (Decree-Law 33/2021) differ from KSA (Labor Law art 83)?" → "Both up to 2 years; UAE requires proportionality (Cabinet Decision 1/2022); KSA more textual"

# Spaced repetition algorithm
- New cards: see daily
- Cards correct twice: see in 3 days
- Correct three times: see in 1 week
- Correct four times: see in 1 month
- Wrong: reset

# Export
- Anki-compatible (.apkg)
- Plain CSV (Anki, Quizlet, RemNote)
- In-app review (Justinian)
