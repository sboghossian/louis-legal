# CBUAE — Central Bank of the UAE — Banking Regulation Quick Reference

## CBUAE overview
- **Central Bank of the UAE** — UAE Federal central bank
- Established 1980; renewed mandate under Federal Decree-Law 14/2018
- Banking regulator + monetary policy

## Regulated entities (onshore UAE)
- Commercial banks (~50 licensed)
- Investment banks
- Finance companies
- Insurance companies (joint with Insurance Authority pre-2021 merger)
- Exchange houses
- Payment service providers
- Stored Value Facility (SVF) issuers

## Free zones (separate regulation)
- **DIFC** → DFSA regulates
- **ADGM** → FSRA regulates
- Outside CBUAE jurisdiction for most purposes

## Banking license types
- **Commercial bank**
- **Investment bank**
- **Islamic bank**
- **Specialized financial institution**
- **Branch of foreign bank**

## Capital requirements
- Commercial bank: AED 3 billion paid-up capital
- Significant capital adequacy buffers
- Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) targets

## Prudential framework
- **Basel III aligned** — fully implemented
- Capital adequacy ratio ≥ 12% (CET1 ≥ 7%)
- Liquidity coverage ratio ≥ 100%
- Net stable funding ratio ≥ 100%
- Large exposure limits
- Real estate concentration limits
- UAE national hire targets (Emiratisation)

## Standards of conduct
- Consumer protection regulations (2020)
- Anti-fraud + complaint resolution
- Mortgage rules — LTV caps
- Credit standards

## Islamic banking
- Higher Shariah Authority (HSA) at CBUAE — federal-level Sharia oversight
- Each institution's Internal Sharia Supervisory Committee
- AAOIFI standards adopted

## AML/CFT
- Federal Decree-Law 20/2018
- goAML platform
- Strict enforcement post-FATF grey list 2022-2024
- Sanctions screening mandatory

## Recent reforms
- **2024**: enhanced consumer protection
- **2025**: open banking framework
- **2026**: digital-only bank licensing
- Continuous AML enhancement

## Notable
- **WAM** — UAE Wage Protection System integration
- **CBUAE FX intervention** — managed peg to USD
- **Currency stability** mandate

## Practical for clients
- License application: 12-24 months typical
- Capital deposit + business plan + management vetting
- Substantial UAE presence required
- Substantial Emiratisation requirements
