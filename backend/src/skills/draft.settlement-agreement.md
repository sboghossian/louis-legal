Draft a Settlement Agreement resolving a dispute.

# Required inputs
- Parties (plus any third-party beneficiaries / releasees)
- Underlying dispute description
- Settlement sum + payment terms (lump sum / installments / escrow release)
- Release scope (specific claims / general release / mutual)
- Confidentiality requirement
- Non-disparagement
- Pending litigation (dismissal procedure / consent order)

# Standard clauses
1. Recitals — describe dispute briefly (parties may agree on neutral characterization)
2. Settlement payment — amount, due date, payment instructions, taxation responsibility, late-payment consequences
3. Release — mutual / one-way; specific claims listed AND/OR general release of all claims arising from defined facts
4. No admission of liability — standard clause
5. Confidentiality — strict, with permitted disclosures (tax advisors, internal compliance, court orders)
6. Non-disparagement — mutual; defined scope
7. Withdrawal of proceedings — dismissal with prejudice; consent order; cost orders
8. Compliance with regulators — if applicable (sanctions / antitrust)
9. Tax treatment — settlement is "without tax gross-up" or address W-8/W-9 / TIN
10. Governing law + dispute resolution
11. Entire agreement + amendment + counterparts

# Critical
- **Statutory rights that cannot be waived** in some jurisdictions (e.g., LB end-of-service indemnity; consumer rights in EU)
- **Tax characterization** — settlement of legal claims vs lost wages have different tax treatment; structure deliberately
- **Future claims** — make clear whether settlement covers unknown / future claims (some jurisdictions restrict this)
- **Effective date vs payment date** — release effective only on payment receipt is common

# When to draft vs send for review
A settlement document should always be reviewed by counsel of both sides; this is a "do not draft without sign-off" document.
