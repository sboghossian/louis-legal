# GDPR (EU 2016/679) + UK GDPR — Quick Reference

## Scope
- Personal data of EU/UK residents (regardless of where processing happens)
- Establishment in EU/UK OR offering goods/services / monitoring behavior in EU/UK

## Roles
- **Controller** — determines purposes + means
- **Processor** — processes on controller's behalf
- **Joint controllers** — jointly determine purposes + means
- **Data subjects** — individuals

## Lawful bases for processing (Art 6)
1. Consent (specific, informed, freely given, withdrawable)
2. Contract (necessary to perform contract with data subject)
3. Legal obligation
4. Vital interests
5. Public task
6. Legitimate interests (balancing test required)

## Special category data (Art 9)
- Health, biometric, genetic, race, religion, sexual orientation, political opinions, trade-union
- Requires explicit consent OR specific exemption

## Data subject rights
- Access (Art 15)
- Rectification (Art 16)
- Erasure / right to be forgotten (Art 17)
- Restriction (Art 18)
- Portability (Art 20)
- Objection (Art 21)
- Not subject to automated decisions (Art 22)

## Time limits
- Respond to data subject request: 1 month (extendable +2 months for complex)
- Breach notification: 72 hours to supervisory authority + "without undue delay" to subjects if high risk

## DPO required if:
- Public authority
- Core activities = large-scale regular monitoring
- Core activities = large-scale special category data

## International transfers
- Adequacy decisions (UK, Switzerland, Japan, S. Korea, etc.)
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) - new modules 2021
- Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs)
- Article 49 derogations (limited use)

## Penalties
- Up to €20M or 4% of global annual turnover (whichever higher)
- UK GDPR: £17.5M / 4% of global turnover

## Supervisory authorities
- Each EU member state + UK ICO
- One-stop-shop mechanism for cross-border processing
