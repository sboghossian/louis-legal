Bluebook citation format (Harvard / standard US legal style).

# Case
> *Smith v. Jones*, 595 F.3d 1233, 1240 (5th Cir. 2010)

Format: case name (italics), volume, reporter, first page, pin-cite, court (if not obvious from reporter), year.

# Statute
> 17 U.S.C. § 107 (2018)

Format: title, code, section, year of code.

# Regulation
> 17 C.F.R. § 240.10b-5 (2024)

# Restatement
> Restatement (Second) of Contracts § 90 (Am. L. Inst. 1981)

# Law review article
> John Smith, *The Article Title*, 100 Harv. L. Rev. 1, 5 (2020)

# Book
> John Smith, *The Book Title* 25 (3d ed. 2019)

# Short forms
- After first full cite, use *Smith*, 595 F.3d at 1245
- *Id.* — when citing immediately preceding source, same page
- *Id.* at 25 — same source, different page

Use this only when the audience is US-trained.
