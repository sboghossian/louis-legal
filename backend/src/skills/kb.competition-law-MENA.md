# Competition Law in MENA — Quick Reference

## Saudi Arabia
- **Competition Law** (Royal Decree M/75 1440H 2019)
- **General Authority for Competition (GAC)** — enforcement
- Covers: cartels, abuse of dominance, merger control
- Merger filing threshold + global revenue tests
- Penalties: up to 10% of revenue + criminal sanctions
- Leniency program available

## UAE
- **Federal Decree-Law 36/2023** (replaced Law 4/2012)
- **Ministry of Economy** + Competition Regulation Department — enforcement
- Pre-merger notification thresholds:
  - Combined revenue: AED 300M+ in UAE
  - Asset value: AED 1B+ globally
- Cartels + monopolization prohibitions
- Specific exempted sectors (telecom + utilities under separate regulation)
- Penalties: up to 10% of revenue + criminal for cartels

## Egypt
- **Competition Law** (Law 3/2005 as amended)
- **Egyptian Competition Authority (ECA)**
- Robust enforcement record
- Merger control thresholds (revenue-based)
- Significant penalties

## Lebanon
- **Competition Law 281/2022** — relatively new
- Implementation still building
- Limited enforcement track record

## Bahrain
- **Competition Law (Law 18/2018)**
- Bahrain Competition Authority

## Qatar
- **Competition Law (Law 19/2006)**
- Qatari Competition Authority

## Common patterns
- **Cartel prohibitions** — horizontal price-fixing, market allocation, bid-rigging
- **Vertical restraints** — typically more permissive, sometimes block-exemption-style
- **Abuse of dominance** — predatory pricing, refusal to deal, tying
- **Merger control** — thresholds + mandatory notification + suspensory periods

## Merger control practice
- Filing fees vary
- Review periods: 30-90 days typical
- Phase II reviews for complex cases
- Conditional approvals + remedies (divestiture, behavioral)

## Cross-border deals
- File in multiple MENA jurisdictions if thresholds met in each
- Coordination via joint reviews increasingly common
- GCC-level competition law harmonization slow

## Investigations
- Dawn raids permitted in most jurisdictions
- Confidentiality issues with seized documents
- Privilege scope varies by jurisdiction

## Practical drafting
- Distribution + agency agreements: avoid hardcore restrictions (RPM, absolute territory)
- Mergers: file pre-completion if thresholds met
- JVs: assess full-function vs cooperative (different treatment)
- Information exchange in JVs: limit competitively sensitive info
