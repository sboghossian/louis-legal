End-to-end incorporation pack for a new startup.

# Deliverables
1. **Articles of Association** — [[draft.articles-of-association]]
2. **Bylaws / Shareholders' Agreement** — [[draft.shareholders-agreement]]
3. **Founders' Agreement** — [[draft.founders-agreement]]
4. **Vesting schedules** — [[draft.vesting-schedule]]
5. **IP assignment from founders** — [[draft.IP-assignment]]
6. **Employee Stock Option Plan** template
7. **Initial board resolutions** — [[draft.board-resolution]]
8. **NDA template** for early discussions — [[draft.NDA-mutual]]
9. **Founder employment / consulting agreements** — [[draft.consulting-agreement]]
10. **Privacy policy + ToS** for products — [[draft.privacy-policy]] + [[draft.terms-of-service]]
11. **Bank account opening kit** — corporate docs, beneficial ownership disclosure, KYC

# Workflow steps
1. **Jurisdiction choice** — see [[research.jurisdiction-comparison]]:
   - DIFC for tech VC (English-law, mature ecosystem)
   - ADGM for crypto / digital assets (favorable regulatory)
   - Delaware for US-bound startups
   - Singapore for Asian markets
   - Local (UAE-onshore, KSA, LB) for local-only operations
2. **Name reservation** + name clearance check
3. **Registered agent** + registered office
4. **Articles of Association** drafted + filed
5. **Initial directors + officers** appointed
6. **Share capital** issued (founders, with vesting)
7. **Bank account opening** — registers + IDs + beneficial ownership disclosures
8. **Tax registrations** — VAT, withholding, corporate tax (where applicable)
9. **Employee registration platforms** — Qiwa (KSA), MOHRE (UAE), NSSF (LB)
10. **Initial board resolutions** — bank signatures, IP assignment acceptance, option pool

# Critical first-90-days
- Founder IP assignments executed and recorded
- 83(b) election filed (US) within 30 days
- Option pool reserved + plan documents adopted
- Board minutes for initial decisions
