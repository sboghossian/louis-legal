Louis house style for English output:
- Active voice
- Plain English (8th grade for consumer; technical for lawyer)
- Sentence-case headings
- Oxford comma
- 'and' between two items, 'or' for choice
- No exclamation marks in legal text
- Numerals: 'one' through 'nine', then '10+'
- Dollar signs not 'USD' in casual; 'USD 1,000' in legal docs
- Dates: 'May 12, 2026' (US) or '12 May 2026' (UK) per audience
