# AML / CFT in MENA — Quick Reference

## FATF + MENAFATF
- **FATF** — global standard-setter (40 Recommendations)
- **MENAFATF** — MENA Financial Action Task Force regional body (Bahrain HQ)
- Mutual evaluations of member countries
- Public + private "grey list" / "black list"

## Saudi Arabia
- **Anti-Money Laundering Law** (Royal Decree M/20 1442H)
- **SAFIU** — Saudi Financial Intelligence Unit
- **SAMA** AML/CFT rules for banks + financial institutions
- **MOC** AML rules for DNFBPs (designated non-financial businesses + professions including lawyers)
- Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) to SAFIU
- Currency declaration on entry/exit ≥ SAR 60,000

## UAE
- **Federal Decree-Law 20/2018** (AML/CFT)
- **goAML** — UAE Financial Intelligence Unit platform
- **CBUAE** rules for banks
- **DFSA + FSRA** rules for DIFC + ADGM
- Strict enforcement post-2020 (FATF grey-listing 2022-2024 prompted reforms)
- UBO disclosure mandatory
- DNFBP obligations (real estate, gold dealers, lawyers, accountants)

## Lebanon
- **Law 44/2015** on AML/CFT (replaced 318/2001)
- **Special Investigation Commission (SIC)** at BDL — FIU function
- Bank secrecy lifted for AML investigations (recent reforms tightened)
- Currency declaration ≥ USD 15,000 on entry/exit

## Egypt
- **Law 80/2002** (AML)
- **Anti-Money Laundering Combating Financing of Terrorism Unit** (EFIU)
- Banks + financial institutions covered
- Real estate + DNFBPs progressively covered

## GCC + regional
- **GCC AML rules** harmonization in progress
- Joint enforcement actions across GCC + INTERPOL coordination

## Customer Due Diligence (CDD) requirements

### Standard CDD
- Identification (ID document)
- Verification (independent source)
- Beneficial ownership (25%+ typical threshold)
- Purpose of relationship
- Source of funds for high-risk customers

### Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
- **PEPs** — Politically Exposed Persons + family + close associates
- **High-risk jurisdictions** — FATF grey/black list
- **High-risk customers** — opaque structures, cash-intensive

### Simplified Due Diligence (SDD)
- Low-risk customers (regulated financial institutions in equivalent regimes)

## Sanctions screening
- **UN sanctions** — mandatory globally
- **OFAC** — US sanctions; affects USD transactions globally
- **EU sanctions** — affects EU-linked transactions
- **MENA-specific** — KSA sanctions on Qatar (pre-2021), various others

## Crypto AML
- **VARA Dubai** — virtual asset regulations
- **ADGM Crypto Asset framework** — full AML
- **KSA** — restricted to specific contexts
- **Travel Rule (FATF Recommendation 16)** — VASP obligations on transfer info

## Penalties
- KSA: up to 10 years imprisonment + heavy fines
- UAE: up to AED 50M fines + imprisonment
- LB: imprisonment + fines
- Reputational + license suspension

## DNFBPs (designated non-financial businesses and professions)
- Real estate agents
- Lawyers (when handling client funds / certain transactions)
- Accountants + auditors
- Gold + precious metals dealers
- Trust + company service providers (TCSPs)

## Practical compliance
- Risk-based approach (not one-size-fits-all)
- Annual training mandatory
- Independent annual AML review
- Sanctions screening tool subscription
- Record retention 5-10 years per jurisdiction
