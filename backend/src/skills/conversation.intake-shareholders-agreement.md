Before drafting a Shareholders' Agreement (SHA), gather:

1. **Company** — name, jurisdiction of incorporation, type (LLC/SAL/FZE/Ltd/Inc), share capital
2. **Shareholders** — names, share counts, ownership %, contribution type (cash/in-kind/services)
3. **Governance** — board size, voting thresholds, reserved matters list (which decisions need unanimity vs majority)
4. **Transfer restrictions** — ROFR, ROFO, drag-along, tag-along, lock-up, permitted transfers
5. **Exit mechanics** — IPO trigger, drag-along threshold, put/call options, deadlock resolution
6. **Special rights** — preferred shares with liquidation preference, anti-dilution, board seats
7. **Restrictions** — non-compete on shareholders, confidentiality

Critical for VC rounds: pre-money / post-money valuation, option pool sizing pre/post-money, conversion mechanics.
