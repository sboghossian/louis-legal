Review a lease for the tenant. Emphasize tenant-protective positions.

# Tenant priorities (push back if missing)
- **Rent caps** — escalation tied to CPI or fixed, not unilateral landlord decision
- **Service charge transparency** — actual cost basis, capped year-over-year increases, audit right
- **Quiet enjoyment** — landlord's positive obligation
- **Reasonable consent standard** — landlord consent for subletting / changes "shall not be unreasonably withheld"
- **Repair allocation** — major structural to landlord; reasonable interior to tenant; HVAC / lifts negotiated
- **Make-good** — fair-wear-and-tear exclusion; if tenant added fit-out, may negotiate make-good cap
- **Termination triggers** — landlord breaches with cure period; tenant force majeure relief
- **Insurance** — building insurance on landlord; contents on tenant; cross-waiver of subrogation
- **Renewal options** — tenant unilateral renewal at market rent (capped) or CPI
- **Pas de porte / goodwill** — LB / FR civil-law jurisdictions: protect tenant's goodwill on non-renewal

# Red flags
- Rent escalation: open-ended landlord discretion
- Forfeiture: no cure period
- Assignment: absolute prohibition
- Indemnity: tenant indemnifies landlord broadly for property-related claims
- Service charge: no cap, no audit, includes capex
- Personal guarantee from tenant's directors
