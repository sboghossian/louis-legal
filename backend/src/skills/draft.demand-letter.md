Draft a demand letter — formal pre-litigation correspondence demanding action or cessation.

# Required inputs
- Sender (party making demand)
- Recipient
- Underlying facts / chronology
- Specific demand (payment, performance, cessation, retraction)
- Deadline for response (typically 7-30 days)
- Consequence of non-compliance (litigation, arbitration, regulatory complaint)

# Standard structure
1. Heading: Sender details, Date, Recipient details
2. Subject: brief description ("RE: Outstanding Invoice #XX-YY — Demand for Payment")
3. Salutation
4. Statement of facts (chronological, specific, factual — no argument yet)
5. Legal basis (statute, contract clause, common-law principle)
6. The demand (precise: amount, action, deadline)
7. Consequence of non-compliance (specific: filing within X days; arbitration; regulatory complaint)
8. Reservation of rights ("This letter is without prejudice to any further rights, including punitive damages, costs, and interest.")
9. Sign-off

# Jurisdiction-specific
- **LB**: "mise en demeure" form may have specific notarial/bailiff service requirements depending on action contemplated
- **KSA**: Notice often via licensed lawyer; bailiff service for court-bound matters
- **UAE**: Notarized demand letter via Notary Public common for commercial matters; required precondition to certain court actions

# Tone
- Professional, factual, not inflammatory
- One-time courtesy: this is a formal record, future court may see this
- Avoid threats beyond legitimate legal recourse
