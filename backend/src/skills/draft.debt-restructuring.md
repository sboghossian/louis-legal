Debt restructuring:
- Identification of existing debts + creditors
- Restructured terms (extension, rate change, debt-to-equity, haircuts)
- New security / collateral
- Standstill provisions
- Inter-creditor priority
- Conditions precedent
- Closing mechanics

Often required pre-insolvency or during distressed M&A.
