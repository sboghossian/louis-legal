Draft a Share Purchase Agreement for a sale of shares.

# Required inputs
- Seller(s), Buyer(s)
- Target company
- Number / class of shares + percentage of capital
- Consideration (cash / shares / mix / earn-out)
- Closing mechanics (escrow / signing-to-closing gap / conditions precedent)

# Structure
1. Definitions
2. Sale and Purchase — share description, consideration mechanics, working capital adjustment if applicable
3. Conditions precedent (regulatory, third-party, due diligence outcomes)
4. Pre-closing covenants (operate in ordinary course; no leakage; access to information)
5. Closing actions (deliveries, signing)
6. Reps & warranties — extensive: title, capacity, authorisation, financial statements, taxes, employees, IP, litigation, compliance, material contracts, real estate, environmental
7. Indemnification — caps (typically 10-25% of consideration), baskets (de minimis + tipping/threshold), time limits (12-36 months general; longer for taxes/IP/fundamental)
8. Specific indemnities for known issues
9. Tax — pre-closing taxes Seller; post-closing Buyer
10. Restrictive covenants (non-compete / non-solicit on Seller, typically 2 years)
11. Termination rights (MAC, breach, regulatory failure)
12. Governing law + dispute resolution (arbitration commonly preferred for cross-border)
13. Boilerplate

# Reps & warranties
- Fundamental warranties (title, capacity): uncapped or capped at consideration
- Operational warranties: capped at 10-25%
- Tax warranties: 7-year survival in most MENA jurisdictions
- IP, employees, environmental: tailored caps + time limits

See [[draft.asset-purchase-agreement]] for asset deals (different structure).
