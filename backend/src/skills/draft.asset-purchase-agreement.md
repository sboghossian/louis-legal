Draft an Asset Purchase Agreement transferring specific assets (and assumed liabilities) rather than whole company shares.

# When asset deal vs share deal
- **Asset deal**: Buyer picks specific assets; leaves liabilities behind (except those assumed); fresh tax basis; more complex transfer mechanics
- **Share deal**: Buyer acquires entire entity with all assets and liabilities; simpler transfer; carried tax basis

# Required inputs
- Buyer + Seller
- Assets to be acquired (precise list)
- Excluded assets
- Assumed liabilities
- Excluded liabilities
- Purchase price + allocation across asset categories (tax-relevant)
- Employee transfer mechanism

# Critical structural elements
1. **Asset list** — granular: real estate, equipment, IP, contracts, inventory, AR, books/records, goodwill
2. **Excluded assets** — defined cash balances, certain contracts, prior litigation
3. **Assumed liabilities** — Buyer assumes specified liabilities only
4. **Excluded liabilities** — taxes pre-closing, pre-closing litigation, undisclosed liabilities
5. **Purchase price allocation** — assets categorized (each has different tax treatment); Form 8594 in US; jurisdiction-specific
6. **Contract assignment** — most contracts need third-party consent for assignment
7. **Employee transfer** — Buyer hires (TUPE / ARD in EU; specific MENA labor law transfer rules)
8. **IP assignment** — recordation with relevant IP offices (USPTO, EUIPO, GCC, etc.)
9. **Real estate transfer** — separate deeds + recordation
10. **Bulk sales notices** — required in some US states

# Reps & warranties
Similar to SPA but tailored to assets:
- Title to assets (clean, no encumbrances)
- Condition of assets (working order, except as disclosed)
- IP ownership + non-infringement
- Material contracts assignability
- Employees - severance exposure
- Compliance with law
- Taxes (pre-closing)
- Litigation

# Indemnification
- Often broader than share deal (less due diligence on entity)
- Caps and survival periods similar

# Closing mechanics
- Bills of sale per asset category
- IP assignments + recordation filings
- Real estate deeds
- Contract assignment + assumption agreements
- Employee transfer agreements
- Escrow agreement
