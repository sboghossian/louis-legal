Fast 5-minute NDA review focused on the points that actually matter for an NDA.

# Quick checklist (output a yes/no per item)
1. Mutual or unilateral? (and does it match what user expects)
2. Defined "Confidential Information" — broad enough? excludes the standard 4 carveouts (independently developed, lawful third party, court order, already public)?
3. Permitted recipients clause — limited to need-to-know with confidentiality flow-down?
4. Term: reasonable (2-5 years typical for commercial; trade secrets often survive longer)?
5. Return/destruction obligation on request or expiration?
6. No license / no IP transfer language?
7. Remedies: injunctive relief in addition to damages?
8. Governing law + forum: home-jurisdiction friendly?
9. Jurisdiction-specific issues (notarization needs for cross-border, e.g., LB Tawqi3i)?
10. Boilerplate present (entire agreement, no waiver, severability, notices)?

# Output
- 🟢 Pass: ready to sign
- 🟡 Negotiate: 1-3 items to push on
- 🔴 Reject as drafted: serious imbalance — go back to drafter
