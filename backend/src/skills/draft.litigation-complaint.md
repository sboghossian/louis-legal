Draft a litigation complaint / statement of claim initiating court proceedings.

# Required inputs
- Plaintiff + Defendant(s)
- Court (jurisdiction + venue)
- Cause(s) of action
- Material facts (chronological)
- Damages claimed
- Relief sought

# Structure (varies by jurisdiction)

## Common-law style (DIFC / ADGM / UK / US)
1. Caption: court + parties + case number
2. Introduction (1-2 paragraphs setting the stage)
3. Parties + jurisdiction allegations
4. Factual allegations (numbered paragraphs)
5. Causes of action (separately pleaded counts)
6. Damages calculation
7. Prayer for relief
8. Verification (where required)

## Civil-law style (LB / KSA / UAE-onshore / FR)
1. To the Honorable Court [court name]
2. Identification of Plaintiff (and counsel)
3. Identification of Defendant
4. Statement of facts (chronological narrative)
5. Legal arguments (article-by-article citation)
6. Claims (numbered relief sought)
7. Annexed exhibits list
8. Signature + date

# Critical
- **Procedural deadlines** — statute of limitations + court-specific filing windows
- **Jurisdiction** — subject-matter + personal + venue
- **Service** — different rules per jurisdiction; bailiff requirements in MENA civil law
- **Translation** — Arabic mandatory for onshore MENA courts; certified translation of exhibits
- **Court fees** — usually % of amount in dispute; verify before filing

# Drafting tips
- Lead with strongest cause of action
- Plead each material fact with date and source
- Quantify damages early (or reserve, with sufficient detail)
- Reserve right to amend
- Attach key documents as exhibits
