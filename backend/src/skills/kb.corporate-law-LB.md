# Lebanon Corporate Law — Quick Reference

## Primary sources
- Code of Commerce (Commercial Code 1942 as amended)
- Decree-Law 304/1942 (Commercial Code)
- Law 126/2019 (e-commerce + corporate reforms)
- Ministry of Economy + Trade (MOET) implementing decrees

## Entity types
- **SAL** (Société Anonyme Libanaise) — joint stock company; min 3 shareholders; min capital LBP 30M
- **SARL** (Société à Responsabilité Limitée) — LLC; min 3 members (recent reforms allow single-member SARL); min capital LBP 5M
- **Société en Commandite** — limited partnership
- **Société en Nom Collectif** — general partnership
- **Holding company** — Decree-Law 45/1983; tax exemptions, narrowly used
- **Offshore company** — Decree-Law 46/1983; activity outside LB only

## Foreign ownership
- Generally allowed (>2/3 of capital can be foreign for most sectors)
- Restricted sectors: banking, insurance, media, real estate (limits per Decree 11614/1969)
- 51% Lebanese ownership required for some land + real estate beyond statutory threshold

## Incorporation procedure
1. Name reservation at MOET
2. Articles drafted + notarized
3. Capital deposit at bank (sealed account)
4. Commercial Registry filing (Beirut / regional)
5. Tax + NSSF registration
6. Bank account opening
7. Publication in Official Gazette

## Capital + shares
- SAL: par value typically LBP 1,000 or LBP 10,000
- SARL: "parts sociales" not shares
- Bearer shares not permitted post-2019 reforms
- Beneficial ownership disclosure required (AML compliance)

## Governance
- SAL: minimum 3 directors; chair + general manager structure
- SARL: gérant(s) — one or more managers
- AGM annually; minimum quorum requirements
- Reserved matters in articles

## Annual obligations
- AGM within 6 months of year-end
- Audited financial statements (depending on size)
- Tax declarations
- Commercial Registry annual report
- Pre-printed registers (shareholders, board minutes)

## Recent reforms
- Law 159/2020 on commercial registries (digital + UBO)
- Law 81/2018 on e-signature
- Law 84/2018 on capital markets
- Beneficial ownership transparency

## Disputes
- Commercial courts have jurisdiction over corporate matters
- Beirut Court of Appeal for major issues
- Arbitration clauses enforced under New York Convention
