Draft a Shareholders' Agreement for an existing or to-be-incorporated company.

# Required inputs
1. Company (name, jurisdiction, type, share capital)
2. Shareholders (named, with current/intended ownership %)
3. Governance: board size, reserved matters threshold, voting % required
4. Transfer restrictions: lock-up, ROFR, ROFO, drag-along, tag-along
5. Exit mechanics: IPO trigger, drag threshold, deadlock resolution
6. Optional: preferred shares, anti-dilution, special rights

# Structure
1. Definitions
2. Capital structure (current cap table; future issuances)
3. Pre-emption rights
4. Reserved matters list (10-30 items typically) requiring 75%/100% approval
5. Board composition & rotation
6. Information rights (quarterly / annual reports, audit rights)
7. Transfer restrictions:
   - **Lock-up**: founders typically locked up for 3-4 years
   - **ROFR / ROFO**: existing shareholders can match third-party offer
   - **Drag-along**: 50-75% can force sale of all shares
   - **Tag-along**: minority can join majority's sale
   - **Permitted transfers**: to Affiliates, estate planning
8. Dividend policy (often discretionary; may guarantee minimum after a period)
9. Anti-dilution (broad-based weighted average standard; full-ratchet rare)
10. Liquidation preference (1x non-participating standard for Series A)
11. Vesting on founder shares + acceleration on change of control
12. Information / inspection rights
13. Non-compete on shareholders
14. Confidentiality
15. Exit:
    - IPO mechanics (lock-up period; cooperate with underwriters)
    - Trade sale (drag-along trigger)
    - Buy-sell mechanism for deadlock
16. Governing law + arbitration

# Jurisdictional adaptations
- **DIFC / ADGM**: full English-law SHA conventions; combined with Articles of Association
- **KSA / UAE-mainland**: limited-liability company rules from local code; ensure consistency with bylaws
- **LB**: SAL (joint stock) vs SARL (LLC) have different statutory regimes; SHA fills gaps not addressed by code

# Common deal-breaker points
- Drag threshold (founders want higher; investors want lower)
- Reserved matters list (founders want narrow; investors want broad)
- Vesting acceleration: single trigger (CIC alone) vs double trigger (CIC + termination)
- Pre-emption proportionality
