Review a lease for the landlord. Emphasize landlord-protective positions.

# Landlord priorities
- **Strong rent escalation** — CPI + buffer, or market-based reset with floor
- **Comprehensive use restriction** — tenant must operate within license; no change without consent
- **Tenant fit-out approval** — plans before commencement; permits + insurance; make-good on exit
- **Repair obligations** — tenant maintains everything inside the demise except structure
- **Service charge** — full pass-through with reasonable allocation methodology
- **Personal guarantee** — for SME tenants; capped at 12-24 months rent
- **Step-in rights** — landlord can enter for inspection on notice
- **Forfeiture** — fast cure periods, especially for non-payment (statutory minimum often unavoidable)
- **Reinstatement** — tenant restores premises to delivery condition at exit
- **Assignment** — absolute or conditional on consent; tenant maintains guarantor obligation

# Watch for tenant asks
- Cure periods longer than statutory
- Reasonable-consent qualifiers
- Force majeure relief from rent (Covid-era trauma)
- Renewal rights

# Statutory tenant protections (cannot waive)
- LB: protections under Loi sur le bail commercial (renewal rights, goodwill on non-renewal)
- UAE Dubai: Decree 26/2013 + 43/2013 rent caps
- Some MENA: statutory good-faith doctrine
