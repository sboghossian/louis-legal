Draft a promissory note — unconditional written promise to pay a sum on demand or at a determinable future time.

# Elements (per Geneva Convention and most local commercial codes)
1. Designation as "Promissory Note"
2. Unconditional promise to pay a sum certain
3. Payee identification
4. Date and place of issue
5. Maturity (on demand / on a fixed date / at sight)
6. Place of payment
7. Maker's signature

# Optional
- Interest rate + computation method
- Acceleration clause on default
- Cross-default with other obligations
- Joint and several liability (if multiple makers)
- Endorsement to bearer or specific party
- Governing law

# Jurisdictional notes
- **LB / FR / OHADA / EG**: civil-law commercial-code rules (sanad il-amr)
- **KSA**: enforceable; Sharia concern around interest — restructure interest as fee/admin charge
- **UAE federal**: enforceable under Commercial Code; bounced-note exposure (criminal for dishonor in some cases — verify current law)
- **DIFC/ADGM/UK/US**: full bills-of-exchange conventions
- **Negotiable instrument** vs simple acknowledgment of debt — the language matters

# Anti-pattern
Don't omit the unconditional promise — vague IOUs ("I owe X") lose negotiable-instrument status and become only contractual debt.
