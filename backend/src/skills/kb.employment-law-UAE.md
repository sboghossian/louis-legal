# UAE Federal Labor Law — Quick Reference

## Primary sources
- Federal Decree-Law 33/2021 (effective Feb 2022) — Employment Relations Law
- Federal Decree-Law 47/2022 (Corporate Tax — affects pay structures)
- Cabinet Decision 1/2022 (non-compete narrowed)
- Implementing regulations + MOHRE circulars

## Contract types (Decree 33/2021)
- **Definite term ONLY** onshore — indefinite contracts abolished
- Initial term max 3 years, renewable
- Probation: max 6 months
- Auto-renews unless either party gives notice

## Working hours
- 8 hours/day, 48 hours/week
- Ramadan for Muslims: 6 hours/day
- Overtime: 25% premium normal, 50% night, 100% Friday

## Annual leave (Art 29)
- After 1 year of service: 30 days

## End-of-service gratuity (EOSG) — Art 51
- 21 days basic salary per year for first 5 years
- 30 days basic salary per year thereafter
- Pro-rated for partial years (1 month minimum vesting)
- Calculation base: **basic salary only** (excludes housing, transport, allowances)
- Capped at 2 years' total basic

## Notice (Art 43)
- 30-90 days as agreed; minimum 30 days

## Termination for cause (Art 44)
- Without notice/EOSG if:
  - False identity/qualifications
  - Negligence causing material loss
  - Violation of safety
  - Theft / fraud
  - Repeated failure of duties despite warnings
  - Reveals secrets
  - 7+ days unjustified absence

## Non-compete (Art 10 + Cabinet 1/2022)
- Enforceable up to 2 years
- Must be proportionate; courts narrow excessive scope
- Cabinet Decision 1/2022 emphasized geographic + activity proportionality

## Sponsorship + visa
- Onshore: employer sponsors MOHRE work permit + GDRFA residence visa
- 6-month grace period after termination
- Recent reforms: 2-year Green Visa, Golden Visa pathways

## Wage Protection System (WPS)
- Mandatory electronic salary payment
- MOHRE enforcement

## DIFC + ADGM are separate
- DIFC Employment Law 4/2021 + DEWS scheme
- ADGM Employment Regulations 2024
- Choose regime by employer's geographic registration

## Emiratisation
- Private sector quotas for UAE national hires (varying %, sector-dependent)
- Non-compliance triggers fines + service downgrades
