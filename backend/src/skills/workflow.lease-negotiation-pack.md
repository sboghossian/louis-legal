End-to-end lease negotiation pack — for tenant or landlord side.

# Pre-negotiation
1. **Side identification** — drafting from tenant or landlord perspective
2. **Property assessment** — comparable rents, market trends
3. **Tenant due diligence** (if landlord) — financial health, references
4. **Landlord due diligence** (if tenant) — title, financial stability, planning issues
5. **Negotiation priorities** — see [[review.lease-tenant-side]] / [[review.lease-landlord-side]]

# Deliverables
1. **Letter of intent / heads of terms**
2. **Lease agreement** — [[draft.residential-lease]] / [[draft.commercial-lease]]
3. **Inventory + condition report** (annexed schedule)
4. **Premises plan + drawings**
5. **Insurance certificates** exchange
6. **Security deposit / bank guarantee** documentation
7. **Registration documents** (RERA Ejari, Tawtheeq, Ejar, etc.)

# Negotiation axes
- **Rent escalation** — fixed, CPI, market
- **Service charge** — methodology, audit, cap
- **Repair allocation** — structural vs interior
- **Use clause** — narrow vs broad
- **Renewal rights** — tenant option vs landlord discretion
- **Termination rights** — break clauses, notice
- **Subletting / assignment** — restrictions
- **Personal guarantee / parent company guarantee** — for SME tenants

# Pre-completion
- Final inspection
- Inventory sign-off
- Insurance binding
- Bank guarantee in place
- Utility transfers arranged

# Post-completion
- Registration with relevant authority within statutory window
- Initial rent + deposit transfer
- Move-in date confirmation

# Critical
- **Statutory registration** — Ejari (Dubai), Tawtheeq (Abu Dhabi), Ejar (KSA), MOET (LB)
- **Rent cap compliance** — RERA calculator (Dubai)
- **VAT treatment** — commercial leases 5% VAT in UAE; residential typically 0%
