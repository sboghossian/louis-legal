Annual General Meeting minutes:
1. Date + venue + attendees
2. Quorum confirmation
3. Approval of prior AGM minutes
4. Annual financial statements approval
5. Auditor approval
6. Director appointments / re-elections
7. Dividend declaration (if any)
8. Other resolutions
9. Closing
