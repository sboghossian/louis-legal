Draft a cease-and-desist letter to stop ongoing infringement (IP, defamation, breach).

# Common use cases
- Trademark / copyright / patent infringement
- Defamation / libel / slander
- Breach of contract continuing
- Harassment
- Unfair competition

# Structure
1. Identification of sender (with authority — attorney representing rights-holder if applicable)
2. Identification of recipient + identified offending conduct (with specifics: dates, channels, content)
3. Statement of rights / basis for objection (registered trademark No., copyright, contract clause)
4. Specific demands:
   - Cease the offending conduct immediately
   - Confirmation in writing within [N] days
   - Destruction of infringing materials
   - Account of profits (for IP infringement)
   - Public retraction (for defamation)
5. Consequences (litigation, injunction, damages claim, regulatory referral)
6. Reservation of rights

# Tone
Firm but not abusive. The letter often becomes evidence.

# Critical for IP
- For trademark / copyright: state the registration details and identify the specific infringing act
- For patent: be careful — incorrect assertion can backfire (Section 287 / equivalent damages)

See [[draft.takedown-DMCA]] for online infringement specifically.
