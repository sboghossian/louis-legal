# UAE Civil Personal Status Law (Federal Decree-Law 41/2022) — Quick Reference

## Scope
- **Non-Muslim residents of UAE** can opt for civil-law personal status
- Optional: parties may still choose Sharia-court application
- Muslims: still governed by Federal Law 28/2005 (Personal Status Law) based on Sharia

## Key innovation
This is the **first comprehensive civil personal status framework in the Gulf** for non-Muslims. Brings UAE in line with civil-law norms for non-Muslim community.

## Marriage
- Two consenting adults (no minimum age stated; consistent with majority elsewhere)
- Civil marriage at registered authority (Ministry of Justice / Abu Dhabi Judicial Department / Dubai Courts)
- Witnesses required
- Same-sex marriage not addressed (effectively not permitted)

## Divorce
- **No-fault divorce** — either party can file
- Civil-law approach: no need to prove fault
- Court grants divorce upon application
- Optional reconciliation period

## Property division
- Default: equal division of marital property
- Pre-marital agreements (prenups) recognized — can override default
- Inheritance separately governed

## Custody
- **Joint custody** by default
- Best-interest of child standard
- Visitation rights for non-custodial parent
- Parental alienation specifically addressed

## Inheritance
- **Testator has freedom** to bequeath assets
- Default: equal division between heirs if no will
- Sharia rules do NOT apply to non-Muslims under this law
- Cross-border heritability simplified

## Pre-nuptial / post-nuptial agreements
- Recognized + enforceable
- Should be drafted with civil-law principles
- Notarized for evidentiary strength

## Application
- Both parties non-Muslim residents of UAE
- Apply directly to civil court (not Sharia court)
- For Muslims who want civil framework: limited — typically requires both being non-Muslim

## Abu Dhabi pioneer
- Abu Dhabi has its own civil personal status court (set up 2021)
- Federal Decree 41/2022 federalized the approach
- Handles civil marriages, divorces, inheritance for non-Muslims

## DIFC + ADGM
- DIFC Wills Service Centre — non-Muslim residents register wills with English-law principles
- ADGM separately allows non-Muslim wills
- Inheritance routed through DIFC Courts / ADGM Courts

## Practical implications
- **Estate planning**: non-Muslim UAE residents have clear civil-law alternative
- **International divorces**: easier recognition abroad
- **Mixed-religion couples**: navigation between civil + Sharia options
- **Long-term residents**: significant improvement for non-Muslim expat community

## Drafting documents
- Marriage contracts: civil-law form (not Islamic marriage contract)
- Wills: explicit choice of UAE civil personal status law
- Divorce: civil court filings (not Sharia court)
