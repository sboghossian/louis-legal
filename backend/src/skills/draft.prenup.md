Draft a prenuptial agreement — pre-marriage contract on property division and obligations.

# Required inputs
- Both prospective spouses (full identification)
- Jurisdiction (intended marital domicile and any property situs)
- Religion / confession (controls personal-status court application)
- Assets owned by each party (full disclosure)
- Anticipated children
- Specific provisions wanted

# Jurisdictional reality — heavily fragmented
**Strong recognition** (enforceable with proper procedure):
- DIFC, ADGM, UK, most US states, France (contrat de mariage), Germany
- Common-law with civil-law contractual freedom

**Limited / variable recognition**:
- **LB**: each confession's personal-status court determines validity; civil-law-style prenups rarely enforced for religious-court divorces
- **KSA**: Sharia governs Muslims; prenup terms cannot override Sharia inheritance rules; can govern marital property mechanics in some cases
- **UAE**: Federal Law 41/2022 allows civil-law personal-status for non-Muslims; Muslims default to Sharia

# Recommended content
1. Identification of parties + capacity declaration
2. Recitals — intent to marry; disclosure of assets
3. Asset disclosure schedule (separately attached, signed)
4. Property division: separate vs community vs hybrid
5. Treatment of premarital property (typically separate)
6. Treatment of post-marital property (income, investments, gifts, inheritances)
7. Spousal support / maintenance on dissolution
8. Estate planning provisions (often defer to wills)
9. Provisions for children (typically cannot waive parental obligations)
10. Choice of law + forum
11. Severability + amendment procedures
12. Notarization + witness requirements

# Procedural protections (improve enforceability)
- **Full disclosure** of all assets by both parties
- **Independent legal counsel** for each party
- **Voluntariness** — no duress; signed well before wedding (not the day before)
- **Reasonable fairness** — courts review for unconscionability
- **Specific notarization** per jurisdiction

# Anti-pattern
- **Cannot waive child support / custody** — these are subject to court review for child's best interest
- Sham assets disclosures = invalidation
- Pressuring signature in days before wedding = risk of duress claim
