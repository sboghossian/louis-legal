Draft a Request for Arbitration to initiate institutional arbitration proceedings.

# Required inputs
- Claimant + Respondent
- Arbitration agreement reference (clause + governing contract)
- Institutional rules (DIAC, DIFC-LCIA, ICC, LCIA, SIAC, ICSID)
- Seat of arbitration
- Language
- Number of arbitrators
- Nature of dispute + relief sought
- Filing fee

# Standard content per major rules

## DIAC (Dubai International Arbitration Centre)
- File via DIAC online portal
- Article 4 DIAC Rules 2022: identification, dispute summary, relief, arbitration clause copy, arbitrator nomination
- Filing fee: scale based on amount in dispute

## ICC
- Article 4 ICC Rules 2021
- File with ICC Secretariat (Paris HQ or regional offices)
- Statement of claim's identification, dispute summary, nominated arbitrator, request fee

## DIFC-LCIA (now LCIA DIFC seat)
- Article 1 LCIA Rules 2020
- Submit to LCIA Registrar
- Specific to Dubai disputes

## LCIA
- Article 1 LCIA Rules 2020
- Submit to LCIA Registrar (London)

# Structure
1. Identification of parties
2. Reference to arbitration agreement (attach contract + clause)
3. Summary of dispute (chronological facts + legal basis for claims)
4. Relief sought (specific monetary + non-monetary)
5. Arbitrator nomination (if rules call for it)
6. Seat + language + procedural matters
7. Filing fee tender / proof of payment
8. Signature + service of notice on Respondent

# Critical
- **Filing fee paid + proof attached** — incomplete filings rejected
- **Service on Respondent** — usually same day as filing; comply with rules' service requirements
- **Time-bar / statute of limitations** — verify the arbitration clause's own limitation period and underlying contract's
- **Interim relief** — separate procedure may be needed for urgent measures (emergency arbitrator under most modern rules)
