# KSA Fintech Licensing — Quick Reference

## Regulators
- **CMA** — Capital Market Authority (securities, crowdfunding, fintech investment)
- **SAMA** — Saudi Central Bank (banking, payments, money services)
- **MOC** — Ministry of Commerce (consumer protection, e-commerce)
- **CITC** — Communications + IT Commission (telecom + data infrastructure)

## Fintech license types

### SAMA-regulated
- **Payment Services Provider** — issuance, acquiring, money transfer
- **E-money Institution** — store-of-value e-money
- **Money Exchange + Remittance**
- **Banking activities** — full bank license (high bar)

### CMA-regulated
- **Equity Crowdfunding** — investment platform license
- **Robo-Advisor** — automated investment advice
- **Securities Broker** — including crypto-securities (limited)
- **Investment Management**

### Joint regulation
- **Open banking** — joint SAMA + CMA + ZATCA framework
- **Buy-Now-Pay-Later** — SAMA-supervised

## SAMA Fintech Sandbox
- Innovation testing environment
- 12-24 months for product testing
- Limited customer base + transaction volumes
- Approval-to-graduate to full license

## Capital requirements (selected)
- E-money institution: SAR 5-10M
- Payment Services: SAR 5-50M depending on tier
- Robo-advisor (CMA): SAR 5M+

## Vision 2030 alignment
- Fintech is a key Vision 2030 priority sector
- Reduced cash dependence; digital-first goals
- Regulatory sandbox actively used
- Fintech Saudi initiative

## Saudization (Nitaqat)
- Saudi national hire targets for fintech entities
- Higher Saudization tier = preferential treatment

## Practical considerations
- SAMA approval timelines: 12-18 months typical
- Sandbox graduation enables full launch
- Specialized fintech lawyer essential — regulatory complexity
- Sharia compliance often expected for consumer products

## Sharia compliance
- Many fintech products require Sharia review
- Murabaha-structured BNPL common
- Sharia Supervisory Board for full Islamic fintech

## Foreign ownership
- 100% foreign ownership permitted in most fintech sectors
- MISA license + KSA entity required
- RHQ (regional headquarters) license preferences
