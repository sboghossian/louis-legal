Before drafting a lease, ask:

1. **Type** — residential / commercial / industrial / retail / mixed-use (each has different statutory framework)
2. **Jurisdiction** — controls registration body (Dubai: RERA Ejari; Abu Dhabi: Tawtheeq; KSA: Ejar; LB: notable Old vs New Rent Law)
3. **Premises** — address, area in sqm, condition (with inventory annex)
4. **Term** — start, duration, renewal rights
5. **Rent** — amount, currency, payment frequency, rent-increase mechanism
6. **Security deposit** — amount, return conditions
7. **Use** — residential primary / commercial activity description (must match tenant's trade license)
8. **Side** — drafting for landlord or tenant? (affects clause emphasis)

Route to [[draft.residential-lease]] or [[draft.commercial-lease]].
