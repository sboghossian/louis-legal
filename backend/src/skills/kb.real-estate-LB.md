# Lebanon Real Estate Law — Quick Reference

## Primary sources
- Real Estate Law (Decree-Law 188/1926 as amended)
- Old Rent Law (Decree 159/1992) — pre-1992 leases
- New Rent Law (Law 160/1992 + Law 2/2017 reform) — post-1992 leases
- Foreign Real Estate Ownership Law (Decree 11614/1969)
- Cadastre + Land Registry regulations

## Ownership types
- **Freehold (ملكية مطلقة)** — full ownership of land + building
- **Servitudes (حق ارتفاق)** — easements
- **Usufruct (حق الانتفاع)** — use + benefit without ownership
- **Long lease (إيجار طويل الأمد)** — up to 50 years

## Foreign ownership
- Permitted but restricted by Decree 11614/1969:
  - Up to 3,000 m² without Decree-Law approval (urban areas)
  - Larger areas require Decree-Law approval from Council of Ministers
  - Maximum total foreign ownership in any caza (district) limited
  - Specific zones (border + strategic) restricted

## Lease law — Old vs New
### Old Rent Law (pre-1992)
- Tenants have strong protections (renewal rights essentially indefinite)
- Rent increases tightly capped
- "Pas de porte" (key money / goodwill) protected
- Eviction difficult — only for owner self-use or major breaches

### New Rent Law (post-1992)
- Default 3-year terms (residential)
- Renewable but landlord may decline with notice
- Commercial lease has separate strong protections (Law of Commercial Lease):
  - Pas de porte
  - Renewal rights
  - Compensation on non-renewal

## Real estate transactions
1. **MOU + deposit** — preliminary agreement
2. **Due diligence** — title search at cadastre, encumbrances, building permits, zoning
3. **Notarial deed** — must be before Notary Public
4. **Cadastre registration** — transfers effective on registration
5. **Tax payment** — transfer tax (typically 2-5% of value)
6. **Updated title issued**

## Construction
- Building permit from municipality + Order of Engineers
- FIDIC contracts common for major projects
- Defects liability — 10 years (décennale) per Civil Code
- Architect + contractor professional liability insurance recommended

## Disputes
- Real estate courts have specialized jurisdiction
- Rent disputes: specific magistracy in some districts
- Construction defects: engineering experts + courts

## Tax
- Annual property tax (مال الأملاك المبنية) — based on rental value
- Transfer tax on sale (typically 2-5%)
- Inheritance + gift tax on property transfers
- Capital gains may apply on commercial properties
