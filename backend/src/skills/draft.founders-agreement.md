Draft a Founders' Agreement covering equity, vesting, IP, roles, and exit between co-founders before / at incorporation.

# Required inputs
1. **Founders** — full names, current equity split, role (CEO/CTO/etc.)
2. **Company** — name (or "to be incorporated as"), jurisdiction
3. **Equity split rationale** — capital contributions, time commitments, IP brought
4. **Vesting** — typical 4 years with 1-year cliff; reverse vesting standard
5. **IP assignment** — pre-existing vs newly created
6. **Decision-making** — supermajority items, unanimous items, day-to-day operational

# Core clauses
1. Recitals — purpose of company, founders' relationship to date
2. Contribution + equity allocation (cash / IP / services / hours)
3. **Reverse vesting** — founder shares vest over schedule; unvested portion returnable on departure
4. **Cliff** — 12 months typical; before cliff: 0% vested
5. **Acceleration** — single trigger (change of control) vs double trigger (CIC + termination); negotiated
6. **Bad leaver / good leaver** — vested share treatment on departure (good: keep vested; bad: forfeiture)
7. **IP assignment** — all pre-existing + future work-related IP assigned to Newco
8. **Roles** — founder titles + responsibilities; CEO has tie-breaking vote on day-to-day
9. **Reserved matters** — supermajority items: option pool changes, new founders, major financing
10. **Non-compete** during + 12 months post-departure
11. **Confidentiality**
12. **Exit mechanics** — drag-along threshold (typically 50-75%); tag-along; ROFR
13. **Future financing** — pre-emption rights, anti-dilution
14. **Buy-sell** for irretrievable disagreement
15. **Spouse / family** — pre-approval for transfers, including divorce-driven transfers
16. **Governing law**

# Critical
- **Cliff + vesting** is non-negotiable in VC-ready cap tables — investors will require it
- **IP assignment** must be retroactive to ALL work pre-incorporation that's company-related
- **Reverse vesting on initial issuance** — founder receives shares now but unvested portion can be repurchased at par if departure
- **Defining "Good Leaver" vs "Bad Leaver"** — terminate-with-cause, material breach, voluntary resignation before cliff = bad
