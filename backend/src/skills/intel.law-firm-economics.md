Top BigLaw KPIs:
- Profits per Equity Partner (PEP): $3.15M avg (2024)
- Revenue per Lawyer (RPL): $1.2M avg
- Realization rate: 85% (% of billable time invoiced + collected)
- Leverage: 3-4x (associates per partner)
- Margin: 35-45% pre-tax

Mid-size + boutique: lower scale; PEP $300K-1.5M; realization 75-85%.
