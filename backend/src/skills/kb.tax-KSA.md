# KSA Tax Law — Quick Reference

## Authority
- **ZATCA** — Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (consolidates GAZT + General Customs Authority)

## Zakat
- **2.5%** of zakat base
- Applies to Saudi-resident persons and Saudi/GCC-owned entities
- Religious tax + civic obligation; not "tax" in conventional sense
- Filing annual; payment to ZATCA

## Corporate Income Tax (foreign-owned)
- **20%** on net adjusted profit (foreign shareholder share)
- Applies to non-Saudi/non-GCC investors' share of Saudi entities
- Oil + hydrocarbons: 50-85% (specific regime)

## VAT (Royal Decree dated 2017, increased 2020)
- Standard rate: **15%** (raised from 5% in July 2020)
- Zero-rated: exports, international transport, certain financial services
- Exempt: residential rentals, certain financial services, education

## Real Estate Transactions Tax (RETT — Royal Decree M/84 2020)
- **5%** on real estate sale value
- Replaces VAT on real estate transactions
- Exemptions: first-time Saudi homebuyer, some inheritance

## Withholding tax (foreign payments)
- Services: 5%
- Royalties: 15%
- Rent (some): 5%
- Dividends to foreign shareholders: 5% (for some structures)
- Branch remittance tax: 5%

## Personal income tax
- **None for individuals**
- No capital gains for individuals on personal investments

## Excise tax
- Tobacco: 100%
- Energy drinks: 100%
- Sweetened beverages: 50%

## Transfer pricing
- ZATCA TP Bylaws (effective 2019)
- OECD-aligned
- Master File + Local File requirements for groups >SAR 6M turnover

## Permanent Establishment
- Under DTTs and ZATCA guidance
- Service PE common trigger; >6 months presence

## Investment incentives
- 30-year tax holiday in special zones
- Reduced rates for strategic sectors
- Sharia-compliant financing exemptions

## DTTs
- KSA has 50+ tax treaties
- Reduced WHT under DTTs
