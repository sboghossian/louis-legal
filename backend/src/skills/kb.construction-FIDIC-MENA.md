# FIDIC Contracts in MENA — Quick Reference

## FIDIC overview
- **Fédération Internationale des Ingénieurs-Conseils** (International Federation of Consulting Engineers)
- Standard construction contract suite used globally
- Most common contract framework for infrastructure + buildings in MENA
- 1999 + 2017 editions in active use

## FIDIC contract types
- **Red Book** — Construction, traditional design-by-employer
- **Yellow Book** — Plant + Design-Build (contractor designs)
- **Silver Book** — EPC / Turnkey (high-risk transfer to contractor)
- **Green Book** — Short Form (smaller projects)
- **Pink Book** — MDB Harmonised (multilateral development bank financed)
- **Gold Book** — Design, Build, Operate (DBO + 20-year operation)

## MENA-specific considerations

### Common variations
- Force majeure: pandemic + war + sanctions amendments common
- Variation procedure: contracting authority approvals often required
- Time-bar provisions: enforceability sometimes questioned in civil-law jurisdictions
- Payment terms: extended in some markets (40-60 days from invoice)
- Performance bonds: high % required (often 10-15%)
- Retention: 5-10% common; retention bond often used

### Saudi Arabia
- Strong adoption in NEOM, Diriyah, Vision 2030 projects
- ARAMCO + SABIC have specific standard form contracts (FIDIC-derived)
- Standard FIDIC clauses often amended for KSA-specific issues (Saudization, statute requirements)
- Dispute Adjudication Board (DAB) + arbitration popular

### UAE
- FIDIC standard with UAE amendments common
- Dubai Government + Abu Dhabi entities use FIDIC variants
- DIAC arbitration popular for FIDIC disputes
- DIFC + ADGM courts for DIFC/ADGM-domiciled contracts

### Lebanon
- Historic adoption pre-crisis
- Currency clauses post-crisis are critical
- Standard FIDIC less common post-crisis; project-specific drafting

### Egypt
- Common for international-financed projects
- Egyptian Government uses local standard contracts often
- FIDIC adaptation for EGP currency volatility

## Critical clauses in MENA
1. **Currency** — USD denomination vs local currency; conversion mechanism
2. **Force majeure** — broadened for pandemic + sanctions
3. **Variations** — contracting authority approval procedures
4. **Time-bar** — civil-law enforceability of strict notice periods
5. **Liquidated damages** — civil-law courts may reduce as "manifestly excessive"
6. **Penalty vs LDs** — civil-law systems distinguish enforceability
7. **Limitation of liability** — typical 100% of contract value cap
8. **Insurance** — local insurance requirements + reinsurance

## Disputes
- **DAB / DRB** — Dispute Adjudication / Resolution Boards mandatory in many MENA FIDIC contracts
- **Arbitration** — DIAC, DIFC, ICC seats common
- **Local courts** — preferred by some employers

## Practical
- Sub-contracts often back-to-back with main contract
- Insurance + sureties significant cost
- Project banking arrangements (PG, advance payment, retention bonds)
- Performance security amounts: 5-15% of contract value typical
