# SAMA — Saudi Central Bank — Banking Regulation Quick Reference

## SAMA overview
- **Saudi Central Bank** (formerly SAMA — Saudi Arabian Monetary Authority)
- Renamed but retained "SAMA" branding (2020 Royal Decree)
- Bank regulator + monetary policy authority
- Insurance + finance company supervisor

## Regulated entities
- Commercial banks (~30 licensed)
- Investment banks
- Insurance companies (life + general + reinsurance)
- Finance companies (consumer finance, microfinance, real-estate finance)
- Payment service providers
- E-money issuers
- Money exchange + remittance

## Banking licenses
- **Full commercial bank** — broad activities
- **Wholesale bank** — institutional clients only
- **Islamic bank** — Sharia-compliant only
- **Specialized bank** — narrow activities (e.g., real estate, agricultural)
- **Branch of foreign bank** — limited activities

## Capital requirements
- Commercial bank: SAR 250M minimum (recently increased)
- Investment bank: SAR 50M+
- Insurance: SAR 100M+
- Finance company: SAR 20M+
- Payment institution: SAR 5-50M

## Key prudential rules
- **Capital adequacy** — Basel III aligned (CAR ≥ 8%, with buffers)
- **Liquidity coverage** — LCR ≥ 100%
- **Leverage ratio** — ≥ 3%
- **Large exposure limits** — single counterparty ≤ 25% of capital
- **Real estate exposure limits**
- **Connected party limits**

## Conduct of business
- Customer protection rules
- Fair lending standards
- Complaint handling
- Disclosure requirements
- Anti-usurious practices (Sharia-aligned products preferred)

## AML/CFT
- SAMA AML/CFT Rules
- FATF-aligned obligations
- Reporting to SAFIU
- Enhanced for higher-risk segments

## Sharia compliance
- Sharia Supervisory Board mandatory for Islamic banking activities
- AAOIFI standards influence
- Distinct accounting rules for Islamic instruments

## Recent reforms
- **2024**: enhanced AML rules
- **2025**: open banking framework
- **2026**: digital bank licensing (pure-play digital banks)
- Continuous Vision 2030 alignment

## Penalties + enforcement
- Administrative fines (variable, up to SAR 100M+ historically)
- License suspension / revocation
- Director disqualification
- Criminal referrals for severe breaches

## Practical for clients
- Multi-month application timelines
- Detailed business plan + AML program
- Senior management fit + proper review
- Compliance officer + MLRO designation mandatory
