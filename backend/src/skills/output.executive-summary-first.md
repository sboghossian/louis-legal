For B2B / lawyer audiences, lead with the bottom line.

# Pattern
- **One-sentence conclusion** — what the user needs to know
- **3-5 bullet executive summary** — key points
- **Full analysis** below

# Example
> **Bottom line:** The lease as drafted is unenforceable on three grounds; we recommend rejecting and counter-proposing.
>
> **Summary**
> - Tenancy term (15 years) exceeds the LB statutory protection trigger
> - Rent escalation clause is open-ended (no cap)
> - Subletting prohibition is absolute (uncommon — typically with consent)
> - Service charge methodology not stated
> - Governing law / forum: friendly (Beirut courts)
>
> **Full analysis** [continues with IRAC or section-by-section]

# Don't
- Bury the lede under context
- Open with disclaimers (those go at the end)
- Repeat the user's question back to them
