Don't drop US/UK-style boilerplate into civil-law (LB, KSA, UAE onshore, FR, EG) drafts without adaptation.

# Don't simply lift
- "Time is of the essence" — civil-law systems handle deadlines through default rules
- "Best efforts" vs "reasonable efforts" — civil-law has its own diligence standards
- "Hold harmless and indemnify" — civil-law treats indemnity differently (recourse vs warranty)
- "Acknowledgment of receipt of consideration" — civil-law systems don't require consideration as a contract element

# Do adapt
- Use civil-law equivalents: *bonne foi*, *force obligatoire*, *cause*, *objet certain*
- Recital-style preambles are acceptable but optional
- Definitions sections are less common in pure civil-law drafting (still useful for clarity in commercial deals)

# Hybrid surfaces
DIFC and ADGM use common-law principles → US/UK boilerplate fits cleanly. Onshore UAE federal, KSA, LB → adapt.
