Before drafting arbitration documents, gather:

1. **Arbitration clause** — what does the contract say? Institutional or ad hoc? Which rules? Seat?
2. **Parties** — Claimant + Respondent with full identification
3. **Underlying dispute** — facts giving rise to claims
4. **Relief sought** — monetary + injunctive + declaratory
5. **Procedural status** — pre-arbitration negotiation? mediation? cool-off period?
6. **Time-sensitive issues** — interim relief needed? emergency arbitrator?

# Institutional choices
- DIAC (Dubai International Arbitration Centre) — common for UAE
- DIFC-LCIA / LCIA DIFC seat — DIFC seat
- ICC — international neutral
- LCIA — London neutral
- SIAC — Singapore
- HKIAC — Hong Kong
- ICDR — North America

# Critical
- Verify arbitration clause validity (signed by all parties, scope covers dispute)
- Identify any pre-arbitration steps (mediation requirement, notice periods)
- Calculate filing fee based on amount in dispute
- Consider emergency relief separately if urgent
