Draft a commercial lease (office, retail, industrial).

# Required inputs
- Landlord, Tenant (including signing authority for entity tenants)
- Premises (precise area in sqm, common area definition, parking allocation)
- Use clause (permitted use must match Tenant's regulatory license / trade name)
- Rent (base + service charge / common area maintenance separately)
- Term + renewal option
- Fit-out responsibilities (shell-and-core vs ready-fit; tenant works approval process)

# Critical differences vs residential
- Commercial leases have weaker tenant statutory protections — more freedom of contract
- VAT / tax treatment matters (KSA: 15% VAT on commercial rent; UAE: 5% VAT)
- Trade name and use clause restrictiveness directly affects subletting / assignment value
- Goodwill / fonds de commerce protections in civil-law jurisdictions (LB notably)

# Standard clauses
1. Premises with detailed plans annex
2. Use clause (often very narrow — Tenant must request consent to change)
3. Term, renewal mechanism, option fees
4. Base rent + service charge + variable charges
5. VAT
6. Rent review (often every 3-5 years; mechanism: CPI vs market vs fixed)
7. Fit-out: landlord works, tenant works, makegood obligations on exit
8. Repair allocation (FRI lease — full repairing and insuring — common in DIFC/ADGM)
9. Service charge (clear cap / cost basis)
10. Insurance (named perils, named insureds)
11. Alienation: assignment, subletting, change of control
12. Use changes — landlord consent required (not unreasonably withheld is the negotiating ask)
13. Forfeiture / termination for non-payment with cure period
14. Holdover / overstaying clause
15. Quiet enjoyment covenant by landlord
16. Boilerplate

# Free zones (UAE DIFC, ADGM)
Common-law principles; English-style FRI lease norms; arbitration in DIFC-LCIA or ADGM Arbitration Centre.

# LB notably
Loi sur le bail commercial (Law of Commercial Leases) provides significant tenant protections including renewal rights and "pas de porte" (goodwill) compensation on non-renewal. Negotiate carefully.
