What we CAN say to consumers about Louis.

# Allowed framing
- "Helps you understand your legal situation"
- "Orients you to the law"
- "Prepares you for a lawyer conversation"
- "Provides legal information"
- "Knows the rules so you can ask better questions"
- "Drafts a starting template you can review with a lawyer"
- "Translates legal jargon into plain English"
- "Tells you what to expect from the process"

# Allowed outcomes
- Legal understanding
- Legal orientation
- Awareness of rights
- Knowledge of options
- Procedural preparation
- Template generation (always with "review with a lawyer" framing)

# Tone for consumer marketing
- Empowering, not intimidating
- Plain English
- "You" focused (not "users")
- Examples of relatable situations (layoff, divorce, eviction, business start)

# Channels-specific
- **Instagram / TikTok**: visual, situation-driven, plain-language
- **LinkedIn (B2C cross-over)**: more measured, professional
- **Search ads**: jurisdiction-specific (e.g., "help with Saudi labor contract")
- **Email**: warm, helpful, no jargon

See [[messaging.banned-claims-consumer]] for the things we CANNOT say.
