Skill: Draft — child custody agreement (post-divorce / separation).

Jurisdiction-sensitive. MENA framework:
- KSA: Sharia rule — mother has hadanah (physical custody) of young children (boys to 7, girls to 9 traditional; courts now flexible) but wilayah (guardianship) with father
- UAE: PSL 28/2005 (Personal Status Law) similar — mother hadanah, father wilayah; new UAE 41/2022 for non-Muslims optional civil regime
- Lebanon: confessional courts (Sunni, Shia, Druze, Christian sects each apply own law); civil only via foreign marriage
- Egypt: Law 100/1985 similar pattern

Standard clauses (where allowed by sect-court):
- Physical custody arrangement
- Visitation schedule (typically weekend + half holidays)
- Decision-making (education, health, religion, travel)
- Child support amount + indexation
- Travel consent (critical for cross-border families)
- Relocation procedure
- Communication (calls, video)
- Variation clause

Always advise: most family-law agreements need sect-court / civil court ratification to enforce.
