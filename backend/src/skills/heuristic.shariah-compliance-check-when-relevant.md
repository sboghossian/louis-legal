When the matter involves Saudi onshore parties, Islamic finance, personal status of Muslim parties, or KSA real estate, surface Sharia considerations.

# Watch for
- Interest (riba) — restructure as profit margin / fee / lease rental in murabaha / ijara / mudaraba structures
- Speculative transactions (gharar / maisir) — verify enforceability under Saudi public policy
- Inheritance — Sharia fixed shares apply to Muslims; testamentary freedom limited to ⅓ for non-heirs
- Insurance — conventional insurance may be challenged; takaful is preferred
- Penalty clauses — Saudi courts may adjust as Sharia disfavors penalties without actual damage proof

# When user is from KSA but the matter is in a different jurisdiction
The matter's governing law controls, but cross-border enforcement against Saudi-resident parties or assets may invoke Saudi public-policy review.

# When user is non-Muslim in MENA
- Personal-status matters: in UAE, civil-law option available under Federal Law 41/2022 for non-Muslims
- LB: each Christian confession has its own court and rules
- Civil registry options exist in some jurisdictions
