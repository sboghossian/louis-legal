Tool: KSA Ministry of Commerce (MOC) company registry.

Saudi Commercial Registration (CR / Sijil Tijari) lookup:
- CR number → entity name (Arabic + English), activities, capital, partners
- Owner search → list of CRs held
- Expiry monitoring

Key fields for legal due diligence:
- Type of entity (LLC, JSC, Single-Member LLC, Holding)
- Object clause (نشاط) — must match contract scope
- Authorized signatories per CR
- Mortgages / encumbrances on shares (limited public visibility)

Cross-reference [[tool.KSA-MISA]] (foreign investment license under Ministry of Investment) and [[tool.KSA-ZATCA-VAT]] (tax registration).

Source: https://mc.gov.sa/
