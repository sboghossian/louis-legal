# Shariah Finance / AAOIFI — Quick Reference

## What is Shariah-compliant finance
- Avoids: **riba** (interest), **gharar** (excessive uncertainty), **maisir** (gambling), haram industries (alcohol, pork, conventional financial services)
- Profit-and-loss sharing principles
- Asset-backed structures

## Standard-setting bodies
- **AAOIFI** — Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions (Bahrain)
- **IFSB** — Islamic Financial Services Board (Kuala Lumpur)
- **Shariah Supervisory Boards** (SSB) at each institution
- **Higher Shariah Authority** (HSA, UAE CBUAE) — UAE national authority

## Key structures

### Murabaha (cost-plus sale)
- Bank buys asset; sells to customer at cost + agreed margin
- Customer pays in installments
- Replaces conventional loan; "interest" rebranded as profit margin
- Most common Islamic banking product

### Ijara (lease)
- Bank buys asset; leases to customer with optional purchase at end
- Equivalent to conventional finance lease

### Mudaraba (profit-sharing partnership)
- Investor provides capital; manager provides expertise
- Profits split per agreed ratio; losses born by investor

### Musharaka (joint venture)
- Both parties contribute capital + management
- Profits + losses shared per agreed ratio
- Diminishing Musharaka: bank gradually sells stake to customer

### Sukuk (Islamic bond)
- Asset-backed certificates
- Returns from underlying asset, not interest
- Multiple structures: Murabaha sukuk, Ijara sukuk, Wakala sukuk, Mudaraba sukuk

### Tawarruq (commodity Murabaha)
- Customer wants cash → bank arranges commodity trade
- Customer ends with cash; pays bank in installments
- Common for personal loans; some Shariah scholars uncomfortable with this

### Wakala (agency)
- Investor appoints bank as agent to invest
- Bank earns agency fee

### Salam + Istisna (advance / construction)
- Salam: prepayment for future delivery
- Istisna: order for construction; payment in stages

## Compliance markers in drafting
- Replace "interest" with "profit margin", "fee", "rent"
- Asset backing required — no purely-financial transactions
- Avoid late-payment penalty clauses (or structure as donation to charity, not lender's gain)
- Shariah Supervisory Board approval / certification of products

## Document templates
- AAOIFI Shariah Standards (88 standards) — global reference
- Local SSBs may impose additional requirements
- DIFC + ADGM Islamic finance regulations layer on top
