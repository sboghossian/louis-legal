Draft a Joint Venture Agreement for two or more parties pursuing a shared business purpose.

# Two structures
1. **Contractual JV** — no separate entity; parties contract for cooperation
2. **Corporate JV** — new entity (NewCo); parties hold shares per agreed ratio

# Required inputs
1. **Parties** — and their roles (operator vs financial vs technical)
2. **JV purpose** — precisely defined; outside-purpose activities allowed?
3. **Structure** — contractual or corporate; if corporate, jurisdiction of NewCo
4. **Contributions** — each party's contribution (cash / IP / services / market access)
5. **Ownership** — % equity, voting rights, preferential rights
6. **Governance** — board composition, reserved matters, deadlock resolution
7. **Profit/loss allocation** — pro rata to equity? or different formula?
8. **Exit mechanisms** — buy-sell, drag/tag, IPO, dissolution

# Critical clauses
- **Reserved matters list** — what requires unanimity vs supermajority vs majority (capex thresholds, M&A, dilution, change of business)
- **Deadlock resolution** — escalation → mediation → buy-sell mechanism (Texas / Russian roulette / Dutch auction)
- **Non-compete on parties** — within the JV's scope, exclusive vs non-exclusive
- **IP arrangements** — pre-existing vs foreground; assignment vs license; survival post-JV
- **Funding obligations** — initial contributions + future capital calls + default consequences

# Jurisdictional adaptations
- **DIFC / ADGM**: standard common-law JV norms; SHA + Subscription + JV Agreement layered
- **KSA / UAE-mainland**: foreign-ownership rules; commercial agencies act considerations
- **LB**: civil-law société commune; consider impact on goodwill on dissolution
