Draft a unilateral NDA where only one Party is the Discloser.

Use the same structure as [[draft.NDA-mutual]] but:
- Asymmetric obligations — only the Receiving Party has confidentiality duties
- Receiving Party's "Affiliates and Representatives" defined narrowly
- Discloser's IP / data ownership clauses are heavier
- Remedies include explicit acknowledgment that monetary damages may be inadequate → injunctive relief
- Term: typically longer than mutual NDA (3-5 years standard) because there's no symmetric pressure

Use this when:
- Investor pitch sharing (founder discloses confidential business info to VC)
- Vendor receiving customer data
- Employee receiving employer trade secrets (often integrated into employment contract instead)
