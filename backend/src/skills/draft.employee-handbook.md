Draft an employee handbook — codified workplace policies and procedures.

# Standard sections
1. **Welcome + company overview**
2. **Employment basics**:
   - Equal opportunity statement
   - At-will employment / contract type (jurisdiction-dependent)
   - Background check policy
3. **Compensation + benefits**:
   - Pay schedule
   - Overtime
   - Annual leave
   - Sick leave
   - Health/medical insurance
   - End-of-service benefits
   - Performance reviews + raises
4. **Workplace conduct**:
   - Code of conduct
   - Anti-harassment / anti-discrimination
   - Workplace safety
   - Drug + alcohol policy
   - Dress code
5. **Leave policies**:
   - Annual / vacation
   - Sick
   - Maternity / paternity / parental
   - Bereavement
   - Religious / cultural
   - Unpaid leave
   - Reservist / military
6. **Hours + attendance**:
   - Working hours
   - Overtime authorization
   - Remote work / hybrid policy
   - Tardiness
7. **Technology + data**:
   - Acceptable use
   - BYOD policy
   - Confidentiality
   - Social media
   - Email / monitoring
8. **Career + performance**:
   - Performance reviews
   - Training + development
   - Promotion process
   - Progressive discipline
9. **Termination procedures**:
   - Voluntary resignation (notice)
   - Termination (with / without cause)
   - End-of-service procedures
10. **Complaint + grievance procedures**
11. **Acknowledgment + signature page**

# Jurisdictional considerations
- **MENA (LB/KSA/UAE)**: Bilingual Arabic-English required for onshore filings
- **EU**: must comply with Working Time Directive, Posted Workers Directive, etc.
- **DIFC/ADGM**: free-zone employment regulations override federal
- **Saudization / Emiratisation**: separate sections for national hire policies

# Critical clauses
- **At-will disclaimer** (where applicable) — handbook is informational, not contractual
- **Right to modify** — employer reserves right to update with notice
- **Severability** — invalid sections don't invalidate the whole
- **Local law supersedes** — handbook subject to local law

# Anti-patterns
- Treating handbook as contract (unintended binding effect)
- Over-prescription (rigidity hurts when situations vary)
- Missing acknowledgment signature page (no record employee read it)
