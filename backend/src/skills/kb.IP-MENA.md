# IP Law in MENA — Quick Reference

## Regional treaties
- **WIPO** — most MENA countries are members
- **Paris Convention** (industrial property) — most members
- **Berne Convention** (copyright) — most members
- **Madrid Protocol** (trademark) — UAE, KSA, Egypt, Bahrain, Oman, Tunisia, Algeria, Morocco
- **PCT** (patents) — most members
- **GCC Patent Office** — unified GCC patents
- **GCC Trademark Law** — being harmonized

## Trademarks

### Saudi Arabia (SAIP)
- Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP) — established 2017
- Madrid Protocol member
- 10-year renewable terms
- First-to-use OR first-to-file (specific facts)

### UAE (MOEC)
- Federal Decree-Law 36/2021 on Trademarks
- 10-year renewable
- Online filing via MOEC portal
- Madrid Protocol member since 2021

### Lebanon (MOET IP Office)
- Old TM law (1924) + amendments
- 15-year renewable
- File at Ministry of Economy + Trade

### Egypt (Egyptian Patent + Trademark Office)
- Law 82/2002 (Intellectual Property Law)
- 10-year renewable

### GCC Trademark Law
- 2014 unified legal framework — adopted across GCC
- National filings still required in each state (not single GCC TM registration)

## Patents

### KSA (SAIP)
- Patent Law (Royal Decree M/27 1425H)
- 20-year term from filing
- Annuities required to maintain

### UAE (MOEC)
- Federal Decree-Law 11/2021
- 20-year term
- Substantive examination

### GCC Patent Office (Riyadh)
- Regional patent for all GCC states
- Application + examination at GCC level
- Successor to national filing in member states (when granted)

### Lebanon (LB IP Office)
- Patent Law 240/2000
- 20-year term

## Copyright
- Generally life + 50-70 years (varies)
- KSA: Royal Decree M/41 1424H (Copyright Law)
- UAE: Federal Decree-Law 38/2021
- LB: Law 75/1999
- EG: Law 82/2002

## Trade secrets
- Common-law-style protection through contract + employment
- KSA: protected under Unfair Competition Law
- UAE: Decree 36/2021 includes trade secret protections

## Enforcement
- **Civil**: damages + injunctive relief
- **Criminal**: counterfeiting + piracy crimes (KSA + UAE active enforcement)
- **Customs**: border seizure programs at MENA ports
- **Online**: takedown processes vary; platform cooperation increasing

## Recent developments
- KSA + UAE + GCC enforcement increasingly aggressive (counterfeit goods)
- Online IP infringement growing focus
- AI + copyright debates (training data, generated outputs)
- Trademark / domain disputes via WIPO UDRP

## Practical drafting (IP-related)
- Use [[draft.IP-assignment]] for assignments
- Use [[draft.IP-licensing]] for licenses
- File in each MENA country separately (no single regional TM as of 2026)
- Sworn translations for filings (Arabic mandatory)
- Notarized POA for foreign counsel
