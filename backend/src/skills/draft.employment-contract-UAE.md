Draft a UAE federal-onshore employment contract under Federal Decree-Law No. 33/2021 (as amended) and Ministerial Decisions.

# Mandatory for onshore federal employment
- **Definite-term only** (Decree-Law 33/2021 abolished indefinite contracts onshore; max 3-year initial term; renewable)
- Contract registered with MOHRE in standard form
- Hours: 8/day, 48/week (reduced in Ramadan for Muslim employees)
- End-of-service gratuity (EOSG): 21 days basic salary per year for first 5 years, 30 days/year thereafter. Calculation base: basic salary (excludes allowances).
- Annual leave: 30 days after one year (pro-rated)
- Notice: 30 to 90 days as agreed in contract; minimum 30 days
- Non-compete: enforceable up to 2 years with reasonable scope/territory (Cabinet Decision 1/2022 narrowed enforcement)

# Work permit and residence
Onshore: MOHRE work permit + residence visa from GDRFA. State this dependency in the contract.

# DIFC and ADGM are SEPARATE
DIFC: DIFC Employment Law (DIFC Law 4/2021) governs — see separate skill [[draft.employment-contract-DIFC]] (TODO).
ADGM: ADGM Employment Regulations 2024 — see [[draft.employment-contract-ADGM]] (TODO).
Pick the right regime by employer's geographic registration, NOT the employee's residence.

# Bilingual
Arabic mandatory for MOHRE filing. English version common; Arabic controls onshore.

# Salary structure
Standard breakdown: basic / housing / transport / other allowances. Important because EOSG is computed on basic only.
