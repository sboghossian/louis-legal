Draft a Saudi employment contract under the Labor Law (Royal Decree M/51 dated 23/8/1426H, as amended).

# Required inputs
1. Employer (CR number, MOL/HRSD establishment number, address)
2. Employee (full name, nationality, iqama / national ID, qualifications)
3. Role, place of work, reporting line
4. Salary in SAR (basic + housing + transport + other allowances broken out)
5. Start date
6. Contract type: **Definite** (default for non-Saudis, max 5 years; Saudis may be indefinite) / **Indefinite** (Saudis only by default; non-Saudis on certain conditions)

# Mandatory under KSA Labor Law
- Hours: 48 hours/week (40 in Ramadan for Muslim employees)
- Annual leave: 21 days first 5 years, 30 days thereafter
- End-of-service award (EOSA / مكافأة نهاية الخدمة): half-month salary for each of first 5 years, full month each year thereafter. Calculation base: last salary including allowances.
- Probation: max 90 days, may be extended to 180 days with mutual written consent
- Notice period: minimum 60 days for indefinite contracts paid monthly
- GOSI registration acknowledgment (General Organization for Social Insurance)

# Non-compete
Enforceable up to 2 years post-employment, but must be limited in territory and field, and necessary to protect a legitimate interest. Excessive scope unenforceable.

# Bilingual
Mandatory Arabic version registered with HRSD via Qiwa. English may be courtesy translation. **Arabic controls.**

# Sponsorship / kafala notes
For non-Saudi employees, the employer is the legal sponsor. Transfer requires Qiwa procedure. Surface this in clauses on resignation and contract end.

# Saudization (Nitaqat)
Mention compliance posture if relevant to Client industry (employer must maintain Nitaqat rating).
