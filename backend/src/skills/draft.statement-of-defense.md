Draft a Statement of Defense / Answer responding to a complaint.

# Required inputs
- Plaintiff's complaint (claims being responded to)
- Defendant + counsel
- Court / arbitration tribunal
- Procedural deadline (typically 21-30 days; jurisdiction-specific)

# Structure

## Common-law style (DIFC / ADGM / UK / US)
1. Caption + case number
2. Introduction
3. Admissions, denials, and statements of insufficient knowledge — paragraph-by-paragraph correspondence with the complaint
4. Affirmative defenses (each as a separate "First Defense", "Second Defense"…) — statute of limitations, lack of jurisdiction, accord and satisfaction, force majeure, etc.
5. Counterclaims (if any)
6. Prayer for relief (typically: dismiss, costs)

## Civil-law style (LB / KSA / UAE-onshore / FR)
1. To the Honorable Court
2. Identification of Defendant + counsel
3. Procedural and jurisdictional objections (must be raised first, often "preliminary defenses")
4. Substantive defenses with statute citations
5. Response to facts paragraph-by-paragraph
6. Counterclaims
7. Annexed exhibits

# Tactical considerations
- **Don't admit more than necessary** — careful drafting on each paragraph
- **Affirmative defenses must be raised** — failure to plead may waive them (in some jurisdictions)
- **Counterclaims** — assess strategic value before joining; they add complexity but can shift settlement leverage
- **Jurisdiction objections** — raise first, separately; some jurisdictions require this to preserve

# Deadlines
- LB civil: 30 days from service
- UAE federal: 15-30 days depending on court
- DIFC: 28 days standard
- ADGM: 28 days standard
- KSA Najiz: 15 days standard
