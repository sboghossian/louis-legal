# KSA Labor Law — Quick Reference

## Primary sources
- Labor Law (Royal Decree M/51 dated 23/8/1426H, as amended including 2015 + 2019 reforms)
- Ministerial decisions and HRSD (Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development) circulars
- GOSI (General Organization for Social Insurance) regulations

## Working hours
- Max 8 hours/day, 48 hours/week
- Ramadan for Muslim workers: 6 hours/day, 36 hours/week
- Overtime: 50% premium

## Probation
- Max 90 days (extendable to 180 with mutual written consent)

## Annual leave (Art 109)
- First 5 years: 21 days
- After 5 years: 30 days

## Contract types
- **Definite term** — default for non-Saudis (max 5 years)
- **Indefinite** — Saudis by default; non-Saudis only by certain conditions
- 2023 reforms: definite contract auto-renews on continued performance

## Notice periods
- Indefinite paid monthly: 60 days minimum (Art 76)
- Other arrangements vary
- Either party may give notice

## End-of-service award (Art 84-85)
- Half-month salary per year for first 5 years
- Full month per year thereafter
- On termination by employer: full
- On resignation:
  - <2 years: forfeit
  - 2-5 years: 1/3
  - 5-10 years: 2/3
  - 10+ years: full

## Termination for cause (Art 80)
- Without compensation/notice if employee:
  - Assaults employer / supervisor
  - Performs duties fraudulently / in bad faith
  - Reveals trade secrets
  - Repeated failures despite warnings
  - Absent 30 days/year or 15 consecutive days
  - Counterfeit qualifications

## Saudization (Nitaqat)
- Employers must meet Saudi national hire ratios per industry tier
- Non-compliance → suspension of visa quotas + service degradation

## Sponsorship reform
- 2021+ reforms allow worker mobility under Qiwa
- Employer remains sponsor for visa/residency
- Iqama transfer + exit re-entry independent of employer in some cases

## Non-compete
- Enforceable up to 2 years (Art 83)
- Scope must be reasonable

## Compliance platforms
- **Qiwa** — labor relations
- **Mudad** — wage protection / payroll
- **GOSI** — social insurance
