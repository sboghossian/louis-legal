Draft a Privacy Policy — public-facing statement of how personal data is processed.

# Required inputs
- Operator + jurisdiction of incorporation
- Applicable laws (GDPR, UK GDPR, KSA PDPL, UAE PDPL, EG PDPL, etc.)
- Categories of personal data collected
- Sources (user-provided, automatic, third-party)
- Processing purposes + legal bases
- Sharing (subprocessors, group entities, governmental)
- International transfers (mechanisms: SCCs, adequacy decisions, BCRs)
- Retention periods per category
- User rights mechanism (access, rectification, deletion, objection)

# Structure
1. **Who we are** — operator + DPO contact (if required)
2. **What data we collect** — itemized by category
3. **How we collect** — direct, automatic, third-party
4. **Why we process + legal basis** — purpose-by-purpose; GDPR-style legal-basis disclosure
5. **Who we share with** — categories + named subprocessors + cross-border transfer notes
6. **How long we keep** — by category
7. **Your rights** — access / rectify / erase / restrict / port / object; complaint to supervisory authority
8. **Cookies** — short pointer to Cookie Policy
9. **Children** — under-13 / under-16 restrictions
10. **Security measures** — high-level
11. **Changes** — notice mechanism for material changes
12. **Contact** — email + postal

# Jurisdiction matrix
- **GDPR (EU + UK)**: explicit legal basis required; DPO if scale; SCCs for transfers
- **KSA PDPL** (Personal Data Protection Law): consent-first; localization preferences for sensitive data
- **UAE PDPL**: Federal Decree-Law 45/2021; consent + balancing tests; cross-border requires adequacy or safeguards
- **EG PDPL**: Law 151/2020; data center localization preferences

# Critical
- **Be specific.** Vague catch-all language ("we may use your data for various purposes") fails GDPR / PDPL.
- **Match your reality.** Don't disclose practices you don't actually do — and disclose ones you do.
- **Update mechanism.** Track effective date; surface material changes to users.
