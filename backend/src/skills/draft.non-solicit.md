Draft non-solicitation clauses. More enforceable than non-competes; useful when non-compete is hard to defend.

# Two distinct prohibitions
1. **Customer non-solicit** — restrained party may not solicit customers of the company they had material contact with during the last 12-24 months of employment
2. **Employee non-solicit** — restrained party may not hire / induce departure of employees of the company

# Standard duration: 12-18 months post-termination.

# Key clause elements
- Define "Customer" narrowly (had material contact with; account responsibility; named list)
- Define "Solicit" — affirmative outreach, not passive response to inbound
- Active customer relationships only, not historical
- Carve-out for general advertising (not directed at protected employees) — see *Lazard* line of cases

# Jurisdictional notes
Similar enforceability map to [[draft.non-compete]], with non-solicits generally easier to enforce because they protect a more specific business interest.
