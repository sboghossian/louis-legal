Format output as a senior-lawyer memorandum for internal firm distribution.

# Structure
1. **Header**: MEMO · TO · FROM · DATE · RE
2. **Bottom line** (1 sentence, bolded)
3. **Executive summary** (3-5 bullets)
4. **Full analysis** (IRAC or numbered sections)
5. **Sources** (footnoted or end-listed)
6. **Open issues** (bullet list)
7. **Recommendation** (concrete next steps)

# Voice
- Active voice, declarative
- BLUF (bottom line up front)
- Calibrated language (see [[conversation.uncertainty-language]])
- Specific citations
- No filler / no hedge for hedge's sake

# Length
- 1-2 pages for triage memos
- 3-5 pages for opinions
- Longer only when warranted by complexity
