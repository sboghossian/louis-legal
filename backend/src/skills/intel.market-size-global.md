Global legal services market — key sizing metrics.

# Headline numbers (recent)
- **Global legal services market**: ~$1.08 trillion (2024-2026 estimates)
- **Avg billable rate (global)**: ~$352/hour (BigLaw partners $1,000-1,500+; in-house $200-600)
- **Access-to-justice gap**: ~92% of low-income legal needs unmet globally

# Segments
- **AmLaw 100** (top 100 US firms): ~$150B revenue collectively
- **Mid-tier firms**: significant fragmentation, ~$200B collectively
- **In-house legal departments**: growing share, ~$100B+ globally
- **Alternative Legal Service Providers (ALSPs)**: ~$25B (fastest growing segment)
- **Legal AI**: $3-5B 2024, projected ~$30B by 2030 (22.3% CAGR)

# MENA specifically
- **MENA legal market**: ~$8-12B (estimates)
- Growth driven by Vision 2030 (KSA), business hubs (DIFC, ADGM), Egypt expansion
- Legal tech adoption accelerating, especially in Gulf

# Drivers
- Digital transformation of law firms
- AI augmentation (skills routers, draft assistance, doc review)
- ALSP growth (commoditized work moves to specialized providers)
- In-house legal departments growing (49% restructured in last 3 years)
- Access-to-justice gap creates consumer-legal-AI opportunity

# Implications for Louis
- Massive TAM
- B2B (law firms) is high willingness-to-pay but slow sales cycle
- B2C (consumers) is large addressable but low ARPU
- MENA-first strategy with Arabic + jurisdictional depth differentiates
- 92% A2J gap = Louis Twin bull case
