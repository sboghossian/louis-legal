AI productivity gains (100x+ on some tasks) have not reduced billable hours. Reasons:
- Pricing inertia (firms still bill hourly)
- Capacity for new work expands
- AI augmentation increases throughput, not displacement
- Client expectations rise (faster turnaround)

Implication: AI ROI accrues to firm margins, not client savings — pricing reform pressure increasing.
