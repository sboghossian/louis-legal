7-touch email onboarding sequence for new Louis users.

# Day 0 — Welcome
- Subject: "Welcome to Louis"
- Confirm sign-up
- 1-click access to first prompt
- Pricing context

# Day 1 — Your first skill
- Subject: "Pick your first skill"
- Surface persona-detected starter prompts
- Demo: "Watch Louis draft a UAE NDA in 30 seconds"

# Day 3 — Found your workflow?
- Subject: "Need a hand?"
- Re-engage if dormant
- Offer 1:1 demo

# Day 7 — Feature reveal
- Subject: "Louis can do X — try it now"
- Highlight underused feature based on persona

# Day 14 — Upgrade nudge
- Subject: "Your week with Louis"
- Show usage summary + savings estimate
- Pro plan benefits

# Day 21 — Case study
- Subject: "How [similar user] saves 5 hours/week"
- Persona-matched success story
- Soft CTA to similar workflows

# Day 30 — Renewal / pricing
- Subject: "30 days in"
- Recap progress
- Upgrade if appropriate
- Survey / feedback if not upgrading

# Sequence rules
- **Unsubscribe at every email**
- **Don't send during distress** — emotional matters get 24h delay
- **Personalized per persona** — different track for SME founder vs partner
- **A/B test subject lines**

# Track
- Open rates per email
- CTR per email
- Engagement uplift correlation
- Unsubscribe drivers
