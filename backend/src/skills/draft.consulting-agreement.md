Draft a Consulting Agreement engaging an independent contractor.

# Required inputs
- Company + Consultant (individual or entity)
- Services scope
- Compensation (rate / structure)
- Duration / engagement type
- Governing law

# Critical structural choice
**Independent contractor vs employee** — misclassification is the #1 risk:
- LB: factual tests; courts look at control, integration, economic dependence
- KSA: GOSI / labor-law tests; sustained relationship with one principal may trigger employment status
- UAE: federal Decree-Law 33/2021 narrowed informal arrangements
- DIFC / ADGM: clearer contractor status if document supports it

# Standard clauses
1. Services description (precise scope; deliverables vs hours)
2. Compensation: rate, invoicing, expenses, taxes (Consultant's responsibility)
3. Independent contractor declaration: no employment relationship, no benefits, Consultant pays own taxes
4. IP ownership: work product assigned to Company OR licensed; pre-existing IP carve-out
5. Confidentiality
6. Non-compete (consider enforceability — see [[draft.non-compete]])
7. Term & termination (typically with short notice for either party)
8. Governing law
9. Boilerplate

# Reducing misclassification risk
- No fixed schedule / office presence required
- Consultant uses own tools / methods
- Multiple clients (don't have Consultant exclusive to one Company)
- Project-based deliverables, not ongoing tasks
- Invoicing rather than salary
- Written contract clearly characterizing relationship

# Bilingual / jurisdiction
For MENA: AR-EN side-by-side common. Always check whether sponsor/work-permit issues require employment characterization instead.
