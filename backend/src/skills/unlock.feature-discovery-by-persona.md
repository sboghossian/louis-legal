Surface different features to different personas during their first weeks.

# Per-persona feature reveals

## Partner (B2B senior)
- Day 1: Doc workspace + matter context
- Day 3: Skills router + observability
- Day 7: Firm KB integration + playbook customization
- Day 14: eFirm features (matter, billing, team)
- Day 21: Word plugin
- Day 28: Mobile app

## Associate (B2B mid)
- Day 1: Drafting + review templates
- Day 3: Skills library exploration
- Day 7: Customize for personal workflow
- Day 14: Drafting board for complex matters
- Day 21: Mobile app
- Day 28: Pro / Business upgrade

## In-house counsel
- Day 1: Vendor contract review
- Day 3: Friday newsletter pattern
- Day 7: Internal client communication
- Day 14: Compliance gap checker
- Day 21: Multi-jurisdictional comparisons
- Day 28: Team rollout pitch

## Law student (Justinian)
- Day 1: IRAC coach + case explainer
- Day 3: Bar exam track selection
- Day 7: Flashcards from statute
- Day 14: Practice essays graded
- Day 21: Moot court rehearsal
- Day 28: Future-career planning

## Consumer (Louis Twin)
- Day 1: First question answered + empathic onboarding
- Day 3: Follow-up matter awareness
- Day 7: When to call a lawyer guidance
- Day 14: Related-issues exploration
- Day 21: Tools catalog (free public tools)

# Critical
- **Frequency**: 1 feature per day max
- **Skip already-discovered** features
- **Allow user-driven discovery** to override
- **Track engagement** with each reveal

See [[onboarding.feature-discovery-tour]] and [[unlock.first-week-progressive-tour]].
