Draft a real-estate sale agreement.

# Required inputs
- Seller + Buyer
- Property description (full legal description, address, plot/parcel number)
- Purchase price + currency + payment mechanics
- Closing date + place
- Title status (clean, encumbered, what to clear)
- Deposit + non-refundable amount
- Inspection / due diligence period

# Standard structure
1. Parties + recitals
2. Property description (with annexed survey, title deed, plan)
3. Purchase price + escrow / deposit mechanics
4. Conditions precedent (financing, title, permits, inspections)
5. Title warranties — Seller's reps about clean title, no liens
6. Inspection rights + repair allocation
7. Closing actions — deeds, payments, registration
8. Risk of loss between signing + closing
9. Tax allocation (transfer tax / VAT / capital gains)
10. Brokerage fees
11. Default + remedies (forfeit deposit, specific performance, damages)
12. Force majeure
13. Governing law + dispute resolution

# Jurisdictional issues — heavily regulated
- **UAE Dubai**: RERA Form F (Memorandum of Understanding) standardized; registration with DLD
- **UAE Abu Dhabi**: DMT (Department of Municipal Affairs) registration
- **KSA**: ownership restrictions for non-Saudis; specific permitted areas (Saudi Land Authority)
- **LB**: foreign ownership restrictions per Decree 11614/1969 (need Decree-Law approval beyond limits)
- **EU**: notarization mandatory; specific transfer taxes per country
- **UK**: Land Registry registration + SDLT (stamp duty)

# Title issues
- Liens (mortgages, judgment liens, tax liens)
- Easements
- Restrictive covenants
- Boundary disputes
- Adverse possession claims
- Off-plan / under-construction (handover obligations)

# Critical
- **Title search** by registered agent in each jurisdiction
- **Survey** to verify boundaries
- **Inspection** within due diligence period; remedy allocation
- **Permits + zoning** verification
- **Tax clearance** from Seller
