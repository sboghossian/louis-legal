Commercial agency agreement:
- Agent's authority + scope
- Commission structure (often % of revenue)
- Exclusivity (region + product)
- Reporting obligations
- Termination compensation per MENA agency laws (KSA, UAE — significant!)
- Bar to assigning agency rights

Warning: registered agency in MENA creates large termination indemnity exposure.
