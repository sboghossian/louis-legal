Franchise agreement standard structure:
1. Grant of franchise + territory + term
2. Franchise fees (initial + ongoing royalties + marketing %)
3. Training + support obligations on Franchisor
4. Operations standards (Franchisor controls)
5. Brand + IP usage (Franchisor's marks under license)
6. Reporting + audit rights
7. Renewal / termination
8. Non-compete post-termination
9. Dispute resolution
