Draft a convertible promissory note with conversion mechanics.

# Required inputs
- Issuer, Holder
- Principal amount
- Interest rate (typically 5-8%, accrues but not paid in cash)
- Maturity date (typically 18-24 months)
- Valuation cap and/or discount rate
- Conversion triggers (qualified financing threshold)

# Structure
1. Principal + interest accrual
2. Maturity — what happens? Repayment in cash OR conversion at then-current valuation OR election
3. Optional / automatic conversion on Qualified Financing
4. Conversion price formula (lesser of cap-implied or discount-implied)
5. Change of control treatment — typically 1-2x return or conversion at cap
6. Events of default, remedies
7. Subordination if any
8. Governing law

# Notes vs SAFEs
- Notes are debt → balance sheet liability until conversion
- SAFEs are equity-like instruments — no maturity / no interest
- Choice depends on jurisdiction (some markets prefer notes for legal certainty) and investor preference
