Template terms for HAQQ partnerships:
- **Reseller**: commission %, territory, exclusivity terms
- **Tech integration**: API access, branding, mutual referral
- **Co-marketing**: content + event obligations
- **Strategic**: equity + governance + lockup
- **Academic**: discount + research + co-authorship
