Legal AI market growth metrics.

# Headline
- **Legal AI market CAGR**: ~22.3% (2024-2030)
- **2024 size**: ~$3-5B
- **2030 projected**: ~$30B
- Fastest-growing segment within legal tech

# Drivers
- AI capability leap (LLMs since 2023)
- BigLaw + enterprise adoption (Harvey, etc.)
- In-house legal departments seeking efficiency
- VC investment surge ($2.3B in Q1 2026 alone for legal tech)

# Major players
- **Harvey** (US, OpenAI-backed) — BigLaw focus
- **Spellbook** (Canada) — Word plugin
- **Legora** (Sweden) — EU contract review
- **CoCounsel** (US, Thomson Reuters) — research-focused
- **Genie AI** (UK) — templates
- **Anthropic Claude for Word** (new entry)
- **Louis (HAQQ)** — MENA-focused, multi-jurisdictional

# Sub-segments + growth
- **Contract review**: 25%+ CAGR
- **Legal research**: 20% CAGR
- **Drafting**: 30% CAGR (largest delta from AI improvements)
- **Compliance + regulatory**: 18% CAGR
- **Litigation support**: 22% CAGR

# Funding heat
- **2025**: $6B legal tech funding total
- **Q1 2026**: $2.3B (acceleration)
- Notable rounds: Harvey ($300M Series C 2024), Spellbook ($30M+)

# What this means
- Window of opportunity 2024-2028 for category leaders
- Differentiation key (vs commodity LLM wrappers)
- Geographic specialization a viable wedge (Louis MENA bet)
- Skills system architecture increasingly important (vs monolithic prompts)
