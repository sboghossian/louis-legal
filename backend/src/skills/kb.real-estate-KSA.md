# KSA Real Estate Law — Quick Reference

## Primary sources
- Real Estate Registration Law (Royal Decree M/6 1423H)
- Real Estate General Authority (REGA) regulations
- Saudi Land Authority (SLA)
- Foreign Real Estate Ownership Law (Royal Decree M/15 1421H)
- Ejar Electronic Registration regulations

## Ownership types
- **Freehold** — full ownership (Saudi nationals)
- **Usufruct (حق الانتفاع)** — long-term use rights
- **Investment ownership** — for foreign investors in approved areas

## Foreign ownership
- Generally restricted — Saudi nationals and GCC nationals priority
- Foreign investors permitted in:
  - **Investment areas** specifically designated
  - **MISA-licensed companies** — can own real estate for business purposes
  - **Premium Residency holders** — can own (recent reform)
- Holy cities (Makkah + Madinah) — restricted further

## Ejar electronic registration (mandatory)
- All residential + commercial lease agreements
- Registered via Ejar platform (REGA)
- Disputes go to Ejar Rental Committee
- Failure to register: contract not enforceable

## Real estate transactions
1. **Letter of intent** + deposit (urbon)
2. **Title verification** at Notary Public + Saudi Land Authority
3. **Notary execution** of deed
4. **Transfer at land registry** (مكتب الأراضي)
5. **5% RETT** (Real Estate Transactions Tax) instead of VAT
6. **First Saudi homebuyer exemption** available
7. **New title (Sakk)** issued

## Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs)
- CMA-regulated investment vehicle
- Listed on Tadawul
- Sharia-compliant structures common

## Off-plan sales
- Wafi platform (REGA) — escrow + buyer protection
- Strict disclosure + completion timelines
- Buyer protection fund

## Construction + development
- Building permit from municipality
- Engineering office approval
- Saudi Engineers Council certification
- Saudi Building Code compliance
- Civil defense + safety inspections

## Inheritance
- Sharia-based inheritance applies to Muslims (fixed shares)
- Real estate divided per Sharia formula on owner's death
- Property partition may require court intervention

## Notable initiatives
- **Vision 2030 real estate**: opening to foreign capital, increasing supply
- **NEOM, ROSHN, Diriyah** — mega-projects
- **Housing Ministry programs** — Sakani for Saudi citizens
