Draft a severance / separation agreement on employment exit.

# Required inputs
- Employer, Employee
- Effective date of termination
- Reason for separation (mutual / dismissal / resignation — affects framing)
- Severance amount (statutory minimum + ex gratia top-up)
- Governing law

# Required statutory components (jurisdiction-aware)
- **LB**: end-of-service indemnity per Labor Code Art 50; notice or pay in lieu
- **KSA**: end-of-service award (EOSA) — half-month per year of service for first 5 years, full month thereafter; visa cancellation timing
- **UAE federal**: end-of-service gratuity (EOSG) — 21 days/year first 5y then 30 days/year, calc on basic salary only; visa cancellation
- **DIFC**: DIFC employment law — different EOSG formula (currently 5.83% of monthly wage per year of service per DEWS)

# Optional clauses
1. Lump sum + breakdown of accrued items (salary owed, untaken leave, EOSG/EOSA, ex gratia)
2. Restrictive covenants — non-compete (with consideration!) + non-solicit (reasonable scope + duration)
3. Confidentiality / non-disparagement (mutual is fairer)
4. Return of company property + IP confirmation
5. Reference letter terms
6. Release of claims — broad mutual release; carve-outs for vested benefits, indemnification rights, accrued statutory benefits that cannot be waived
7. Acknowledgment that employee had opportunity to consult counsel
8. Cooperation clause for transition / litigation
9. Confidentiality of the agreement itself
10. Tax treatment statement (Employer doesn't gross up unless agreed)

# Critical
- In several MENA jurisdictions, statutory end-of-service rights **cannot be waived** in advance. Any waiver applies only to amounts above the statutory floor.
- Non-competes post-employment require consideration in some jurisdictions (FR / DIFC need separate compensation); in KSA / UAE federal they are enforceable up to 2 years with reasonable scope.
- For mass redundancies → additional consultation requirements per local law.
