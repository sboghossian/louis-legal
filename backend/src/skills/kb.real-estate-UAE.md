# UAE Real Estate Law — Quick Reference

## Federal vs Emirate
- Land + real estate regulation is largely emirate-level
- Federal Civil Code provides general principles
- Each emirate has specific registration body + rent law

## Dubai (RERA + DLD)
### Ownership
- **Freehold** for UAE/GCC nationals across emirate
- **Designated areas** for foreign ownership (per Decree 7/2006 designated areas)
- **Leasehold** up to 99 years in non-designated areas
- **Off-plan**: governed by Decree 13/2008; escrow account mandatory

### Registration
- Dubai Land Department (DLD) — title registration
- RERA — regulatory oversight, broker licensing, Ejari (rental registration)

### Rent law (Decree 26/2013 + Decree 43/2013)
- 1-year notice for non-renewal via notary/registered post
- Rent increase capped per RERA calculator (based on market index vs prevailing rent)
- 12-month minimum for rent disputes (RDC — Rental Disputes Centre)

## Abu Dhabi (DMT + Tawtheeq)
- Department of Municipal Affairs handles registration
- Tawtheeq mandatory tenancy registration
- Foreign ownership in specific investment zones

## Sharjah / Ajman / RAK / Fujairah
- Each emirate has own land department
- Foreign ownership restrictions vary

## DIFC + ADGM (free zones)
- DIFC Real Estate Law 2009 (within DIFC jurisdiction)
- ADGM Real Property Regulations
- Common-law principles
- Direct foreign ownership of unit / floor within free-zone buildings

## VAT
- Residential property: zero-rated first supply
- Commercial property: 5% VAT
- Bare land: zero-rated
- Off-plan: 5% on installments

## Foreign ownership snapshot
- Designated areas: outright freehold for non-GCC
- Non-designated: leasehold or none
- Free zones: outright ownership inside zone

## Common transaction structures
- **Sale**: MOU (Form F in Dubai) → no-objection certificate (NOC) from developer → transfer at DLD
- **Lease**: drafted privately → registered via Ejari (Dubai) or Tawtheeq (Abu Dhabi)
- **Off-plan**: developer-buyer agreement with escrow account + DLD oversight
