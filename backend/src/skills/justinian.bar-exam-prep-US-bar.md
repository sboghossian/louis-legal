Skill: Justinian — US Bar Exam prep (UBE + state).

Uniform Bar Exam (UBE) used in most states:
- MBE (200 MCQs, 6 hr) — Const Law, Contracts, Crim Law/Proc, Evidence, Property, Torts, Civ Pro
- MEE (6 essays) — additional subjects
- MPT (2 performance tests, 90 min each)

State-specific: CA, FL, VA have non-UBE exams; some require CA-specific PT, MPRE, character & fitness.

Louis: NCBE-aligned MBE drills, MEE essay grading, MPT timed practice, flashcards, MPRE prep separately.
